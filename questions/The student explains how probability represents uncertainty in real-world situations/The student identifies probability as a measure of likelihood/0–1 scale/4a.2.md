#### Internal Notes

##### Competency

##### Explanation
Probabilities are expressed on a scale from 0 to 1. To convert a percentage to a probability, divide by 100: 35% ÷ 100 = 0.35. This decimal falls within the valid probability range and correctly represents the likelihood of a customer preferring the new design.

##### Domain

##### Distractors

###### Option 2
0.65 — Calculates the complement probability

This targets students who incorrectly calculate the probability of the event not occurring (1 - 0.35), rather than the probability of the event specified in the question.

###### Option 3
0.035 — Divides by 1000 instead of 100

This targets students who understand that a decimal conversion is necessary but apply the incorrect divisor, resulting in a decimal shift error.

###### Option 4
0.53 — Transposes the digits during conversion

This targets students who recognize the numbers involved but make a transposition error, reversing the digits 3 and 5 when representing the probability.
