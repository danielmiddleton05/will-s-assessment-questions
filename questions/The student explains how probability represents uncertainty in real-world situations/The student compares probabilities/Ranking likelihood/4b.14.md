#### Task Description

A medical researcher is evaluating the success rates of three different treatments for a specific health condition. The final probability of full recovery for each treatment is calculated by taking a base success rate and multiplying it by a clinical adjustment factor.

| Treatment | Base Success Rate | Clinical Adjustment Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Medication | 7 out of 20 patients | 1.20 |
| Physical Therapy | 3 out of 5 patients | 1.20 |
| Surgery | 19 out of 20 patients | 0.90 |

What is the correct ordering of these treatments from least likely to most likely for full recovery?

##### Explanation
To find the final probability for each treatment, first convert the base success rate to a decimal and then multiply by the adjustment factor:
1. **Medication:** (7 / 20) × 1.20 = 0.35 × 1.20 = **0.42**
2. **Physical Therapy:** (3 / 5) × 1.20 = 0.60 × 1.20 = **0.72**
3. **Surgery:** (19 / 20) × 0.90 = 0.95 × 0.90 = **0.855**

Ordering from lowest to highest probability: Medication (0.42) < Physical Therapy (0.72) < Surgery (0.855).
