#### Task Description

**Given:** A sales manager compares conversion rates for two marketing channels. Channel A converts 15% of leads, and Channel B converts 3 out of every 20 leads.

What is the relationship between the conversion probabilities of the two channels?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Channel A has a higher conversion probability than Channel B.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Channel B has a higher conversion probability than Channel A.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The formats are incompatible for comparison.


###### Option 4 Correct
Both channels have equal conversion probabilities.

##### Explanation
Channel A: 15% = 0.15. Channel B: 3/20 = 0.15 = 15%. Both channels have identical conversion probabilities of 0.15 or 15%, despite being expressed in different formats. Converting to the same format allows direct comparison.
