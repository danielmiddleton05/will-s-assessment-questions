#### Task Description

**Given:** A university registrar analyzes course enrollment. Course A has 45 open seats out of 150 total seats. Course B has 30 open seats out of 60 total seats.

If a seat is randomly selected from each course, which course has the greater probability of selecting an open seat?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Course A, because the ratio of total seats to open seats is higher than the ratio for Course B


###### Option 2 Correct
Course B, because the ratio of open seats to total seats is higher than it is for Course A


###### Option 3 Incorrect
Both courses, because the proportion of open seats is equivalent for both courses


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Course A, because the ratio of filled seats to total seats is higher than it is for Course B

##### Explanation
Probability is determined by proportion, not absolute count. Course B has $\frac{30}{60} = 0.50$ (50%) open seats, while Course A has $\frac{45}{150} = 0.30$ (30%) open seats. Since $0.50 > 0.30$, Course B has the greater probability of selecting an open seat.

##### Domain

Education

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Course A, because the ratio of total seats to open seats is higher than the ratio for Course B — Targets students who use proportional reasoning but calculate the inverse ratio ($\text{total} \div \text{open}$), incorrectly assuming that a higher resulting quotient indicates a higher probability.

###### Option 3
Both courses, because the proportion of open seats is equivalent for both courses — Targets students who recognize that probability depends on proportions but fail to accurately calculate or compare the specific ratios (0.30 vs 0.50), assuming they are equal.

###### Option 4
Course A, because the ratio of filled seats to total seats is higher than it is for Course B — Targets students who identify a correct proportional relationship (Course A has 70% filled seats vs Course B's 50%) but incorrectly conclude that a higher ratio of filled seats corresponds to a higher probability of selecting an open seat.
