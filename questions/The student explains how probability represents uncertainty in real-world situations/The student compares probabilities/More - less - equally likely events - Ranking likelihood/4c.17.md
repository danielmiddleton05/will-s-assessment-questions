#### Task Description

**Given:** A company reviews customer complaint data from four regional offices. The East office received 12 complaints out of 400 customer interactions. The West office received 18 complaints out of 600 customer interactions. The North office received 12 complaints out of 600 customer interactions. The South office received 18 complaints out of 400 customer interactions.

Based on the complaint data, what pair of offices is equally likely to receive a complaint from any given customer interaction?

##### Explanation
Probability is calculated as complaints divided by customer interactions. East: $12/400 = 0.03$. West: $18/600 = 0.03$. North: $12/600 = 0.02$. South: $18/400 = 0.045$. East and West both have a probability of 0.03 (3% complaint rate per interaction), making them equally likely to receive a complaint on any given interaction.
