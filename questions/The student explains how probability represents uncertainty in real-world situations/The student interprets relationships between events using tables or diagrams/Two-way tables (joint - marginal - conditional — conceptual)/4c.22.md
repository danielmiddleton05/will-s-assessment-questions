#### Task Description

**Given:** A college dean reviews data on student performance and tutoring participation using the following two-way table:

| No Tutoring  | 180    | 120          | 300   |
| Total        | 340    | 160          | 500   |


In the context of this data, among students who passed, what ratio describes the proportion who received tutoring?

##### Explanation
The proportion of passing students who received tutoring requires restricting attention to only the students who passed and then determining what fraction of that group received tutoring. The numerator is 160 (students who both received tutoring and passed), and the denominator is 340 (the column total for "Passed"), which acts as the restricted sample space. This gives the ratio 160/340. This represents a conditional relationship: among students who passed, what proportion received tutoring.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
160/500 — Confuses conditional with joint relationship. This ratio divides the 160 students who both received tutoring and passed by the grand total of 500 students. It represents the joint proportion of all students who both received tutoring and passed, not a conditional proportion restricted to passing students. The error reflects a failure to restrict the denominator to only the passing students (340) rather than the entire student population (500).

###### Option 2
200/500 — Confuses a conditional relationship with a marginal relationship.
This ratio divides the 200 total tutoring students by the grand total of 500 students. It represents the overall rate of students who received tutoring, a marginal proportion. The error reflects associating "tutoring" broadly with the row total rather than identifying the specific cell value (160) and the appropriate column total (340) needed to answer the question about passing students who received tutoring.

###### Option 4
160/200 — Confuses the direction of the conditional relationship.
This ratio divides the 160 students who received tutoring and passed by the 200 total tutoring students. It represents the proportion of tutoring students who passed, not the proportion of passing students who received tutoring. The error reflects confusion about which variable is being conditioned on — the denominator defines the restricted group, and the question asks about the group of passing students (340), not the group of tutoring students (200).
