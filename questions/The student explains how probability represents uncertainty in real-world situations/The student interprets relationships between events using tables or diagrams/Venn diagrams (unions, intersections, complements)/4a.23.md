##### Explanation
In a Venn diagram, the area outside all circles represents the complement of all depicted events—in this case, customers who are not in circle A and not in circle B. These are customers who purchased neither product. This concept is fundamental in probability and set theory, where the complement of a union represents elements not belonging to any of the sets being considered.

###### Option 4

This targets students who don't distinguish between the union (inside at least one circle, representing "either or both") and the complement (outside all circles, representing "neither").
