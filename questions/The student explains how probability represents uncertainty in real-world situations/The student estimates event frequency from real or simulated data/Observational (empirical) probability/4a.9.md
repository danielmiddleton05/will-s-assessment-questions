#### Task Description

**Given:** A clinic tracked patient no-shows over 6 months and found that 45 out of 300 scheduled appointments resulted in no-shows.

Based on this data, what is the estimated probability that a randomly selected scheduled appointment will result in a no-show?

##### Explanation
To find the empirical probability, divide the number of observed events (45 no-shows) by the total number of observations (300 appointments). 
$$P(\text{no-show}) = \frac{45}{300} = 0.15$$
This value represents the relative frequency of no-shows based on the clinic's historical data, allowing for an estimation of the likelihood of future occurrences.
