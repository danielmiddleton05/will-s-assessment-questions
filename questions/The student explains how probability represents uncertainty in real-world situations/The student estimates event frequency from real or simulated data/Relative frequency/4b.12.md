##### Explanation
Relative frequency is calculated by dividing the number of times an outcome occurred by the total number of observations: $50 \div 200 = 0.25$. This represents the proportion of calls that were hardware-related, expressed as a decimal.

##### Domain

Technology

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Calculates the relative frequency of the complement ($150 \div 200 = 0.75$). This targets students who identify the frequency of non-hardware calls rather than the hardware calls specified in the prompt.

###### Option 3
Confuses the raw count of "50" with a decimal proportion ($0.50$). This targets students who mistake a frequency count for a percentage or relative frequency without performing the necessary division by the total.

###### Option 4
Calculates the part-to-part ratio (odds) instead of the relative frequency (part-to-whole). This targets students who divide the event count by the remaining calls ($50 \div 150 \approx 0.33$) rather than the total calls.
