Strength of association

##### Explanation
When data points cluster tightly around a trend line with little scatter, the association is described as strong. This means knowing one variable provides good prediction of the other with relatively small errors.

##### Domain

Health

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
A weak association would show points scattered widely around any trend, making prediction unreliable. The tight clustering described indicates the opposite.

###### Option 2
A moderate association would show a recognizable pattern but with considerable scatter. "Minimal scatter" suggests something stronger than moderate.

###### Option 3
No association would mean no discernible pattern. The clear upward trend with tight clustering indicates a strong relationship exists.
