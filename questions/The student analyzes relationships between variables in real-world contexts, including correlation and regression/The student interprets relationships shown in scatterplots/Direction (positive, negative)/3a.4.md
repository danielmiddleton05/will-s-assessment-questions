Positive — Reverses the direction interpretation
This targets students who confuse the visual pattern. A positive relationship would show points moving from lower left to upper right, where both variables increase together.

No relationship — Misinterprets a clear pattern as random
This targets students who don't recognize the consistent downward trend. No relationship would appear as a random scatter with no discernible pattern.

###### Option 4
Mixed — Focuses on point variation rather than overall trend
This targets students who see individual points deviating from a perfect line and conclude the relationship shifts between positive and negative. The overall trend clearly shows a consistent negative direction, even though individual points have some scatter.
