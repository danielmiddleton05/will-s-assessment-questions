##### Explanation
A positive association occurs when both variables increase together. The upward trend from the lower left to the upper right indicates that as the number of miles run increases, the cardiovascular endurance score also increases, which defines a positive direction.

##### Distractors

###### Option 2
Negative — Reverses the direction
A negative association would show one variable increasing while the other decreases (a downward trend). Students who misidentify the slope of the data points or confuse the definitions may select this option.

###### Option 3
Non-linear — Applies an irrelevant concept
While associations can be non-linear, this scatterplot shows a clear linear progression. This distractor may appeal to students who are looking for more complex descriptions of the data without understanding basic directional trends.

###### Option 4
No direction — Ignores the clear upward trend
No direction would be indicated by a random cloud of points with no discernible slope. Students who fail to recognize the visual pattern or have a misunderstanding of what constitutes a trend may select this option.
