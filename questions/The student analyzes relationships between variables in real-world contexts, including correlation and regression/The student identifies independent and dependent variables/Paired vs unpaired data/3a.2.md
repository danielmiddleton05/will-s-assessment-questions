#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital researcher measures patient blood pressure before and after administering a new medication to 50 patients. In this study, what is the response variable?

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student identifies independent and dependent variables.

##### Aligned Content
Paired vs unpaired data

##### Explanation
Blood pressure is the response variable because it is the outcome being measured to determine the effect of the treatment. The researcher is observing whether blood pressure changes in response to the medication. The response variable is what the researcher measures and expects to be influenced by the explanatory variable.

##### Domain

Health

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
The medication administration is the explanatory variable—it is what the researcher manipulates or controls to observe its effect. Students selecting this may confuse the roles of explanatory and response variables.

###### Option 2
The number of patients (50) is the sample size, not a variable in the study. Students may confuse study design elements with the variables being investigated.

###### Option 4
Heart rate is a measurable physiological variable and a plausible health outcome, but it is not the specific response variable identified in the study's protocol. Students may choose this if they identify it as a biological metric but fail to map it to the specific study description.
