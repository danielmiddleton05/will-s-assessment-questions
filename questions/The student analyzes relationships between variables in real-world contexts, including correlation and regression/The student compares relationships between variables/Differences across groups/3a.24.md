#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital compares the relationship between daily walking minutes ($x$) and systolic blood pressure reduction in mmHg ($y$) for two age groups of patients with hypertension. The regression analysis yields the following results:

*   **Group A (Ages 30–50):** $y = 0.4x + 1$
*   **Group B (Ages 51–70):** $y = 0.2x + 3$

Based on these statistics, which conclusion about the relationship between walking and blood pressure reduction is correct?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Correct
For each additional minute of daily walking, Group A experiences a greater reduction in blood pressure than Group B.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Group B benefits more from walking because their starting reduction value is higher.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The relationship between walking and blood pressure reduction is negative for both groups.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Both groups experience the same rate of blood pressure reduction per minute of walking.

##### Explanation
The slope of each regression equation represents the rate of blood pressure reduction per additional minute of daily walking. Group A has a slope of 0.4, meaning each additional minute of walking is associated with a 0.4 mmHg reduction. Group B has a slope of 0.2, meaning each additional minute is associated with only a 0.2 mmHg reduction. Since 0.4 > 0.2, Group A experiences a greater per-minute benefit from walking than Group B. The y-intercepts (1 and 3) represent the expected reduction when walking time is zero and do not describe how the variables change together.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Group B benefits more from walking because their starting reduction value is higher — Confuses the y-intercept with the slope. The y-intercept (3 vs. 1) reflects the expected reduction at zero walking minutes, not the rate of change per minute of walking.

###### Option 3
The relationship between walking and blood pressure reduction is negative for both groups — Misinterprets the direction of the relationship. Both slopes are positive (+0.4 and +0.2), indicating that more walking is associated with greater blood pressure reduction for both groups.

###### Option 4
Both groups experience the same rate of blood pressure reduction per minute of walking — Ignores the difference in slopes. Group A's slope (0.4) is twice Group B's slope (0.2), so the rates are not the same.
