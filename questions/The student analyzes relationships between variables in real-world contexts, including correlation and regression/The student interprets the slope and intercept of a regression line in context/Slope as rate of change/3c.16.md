#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital researcher summarizes the results of a regression analysis:

*"For every additional day a patient stays in the intensive care unit (ICU), the total hospital bill is predicted to increase by approximately $4,200. A patient with zero ICU days is predicted to have a hospital bill of $12,500."*

Which regression equation matches this researcher's description, where Bill is measured in dollars and Days represents the number of ICU days?

##### Explanation
A regression equation takes the form $y = b_0 + b_1(x)$, where $b_0$ is the intercept (the predicted value of $y$ when $x = 0$) and $b_1$ is the slope (the predicted change in $y$ for each one-unit increase in $x$). The researcher states that a patient with zero ICU days has a predicted bill of \$12,500, which identifies the intercept as 12,500. The researcher also states that each additional ICU day increases the predicted bill by \$4,200, which identifies the slope as 4,200. Placing these values into the regression form yields: $\text{Bill} = 12{,}500 + 4{,}200(\text{Days})$.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
This equation uses the correct slope (\$4,200) but incorrectly adjusts the intercept by subtracting one day's cost from the stated baseline ($12{,}500 - 4{,}200 = 8{,}300$). A student who selects this option may misinterpret "zero ICU days" as already including one day of intensive care, and therefore subtract \$4,200 from the intercept to find what they believe is the "true" starting value. This reflects a common off-by-one reasoning error when translating a verbal description into an equation.

###### Option 3
This equation uses the correct intercept (\$12,500) but incorrectly sums the two reported values ($12{,}500 + 4{,}200 = 16{,}700$) and places the result as the slope. A student who selects this option may mistakenly believe the rate of change should incorporate both the baseline and the per-day increase combined.
