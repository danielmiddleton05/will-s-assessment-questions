#### Task Description

A fitness researcher analyzed the relationship between weekly exercise and resting heart rate. The study found that:

- People who don't exercise at all have an average resting heart rate of 78 beats per minute
- For each additional hour of weekly exercise, resting heart rate decreases by 2 beats per minute on average

Based on these findings, what is the predicted resting heart rate for someone who exercises 6 hours per week?

##### Explanation
The baseline (intercept) is 78 beats per minute for someone who doesn't exercise. The rate of change (slope) is −2 beats per minute for each hour of exercise. For someone exercising 6 hours per week: 78 − 2(6) = 78 − 12 = 66 beats per minute.
