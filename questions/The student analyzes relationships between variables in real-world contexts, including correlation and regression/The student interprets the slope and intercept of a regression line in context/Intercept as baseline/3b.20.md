#### Task Description

**Given:** A health app uses the regression equation $\text{TotalCalories} = 1500 + 300(\text{ExerciseHours})$ to predict a user’s daily caloric needs.

What does the intercept value of 1500 represent in this context?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
The average increase in daily calories per hour of exercise


###### Option 2 Incorrect
The highest daily caloric intake predicted by the app’s data


###### Option 3 Correct
The predicted daily caloric needs for a user with zero hours of exercise


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The average daily caloric intake for all users in the dataset

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets the slope and intercept of a regression line in context.

##### Aligned Content
Intercept as baseline

##### Explanation
The intercept is the predicted value of the dependent variable when the independent variable equals zero. Here, 1500 represents the model's prediction for daily caloric needs when exercise hours equal zero, often interpreted as the basal metabolic rate.

##### Domain
Health

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
The average increase in daily calories per hour of exercise — Describes the slope

The slope (300) represents the rate of change per hour of exercise, not the intercept. Students who confuse the two components of the equation may select this.

###### Option 2
The highest daily caloric intake observed in the app’s data — Misidentifies as a maximum

The intercept is not a maximum or minimum value from the data. It is a specific component of the regression equation representing a baseline at X=0.

###### Option 4
The average daily caloric intake for all users in the dataset — Misinterprets the intercept as the mean

Students may confuse the constant intercept ($\beta_0$) with the mean of the dependent variable ($\bar{Y}$). While related in some statistical contexts, the intercept specifically represents the predicted value when the independent variable is zero, not the overall average of the sample.
