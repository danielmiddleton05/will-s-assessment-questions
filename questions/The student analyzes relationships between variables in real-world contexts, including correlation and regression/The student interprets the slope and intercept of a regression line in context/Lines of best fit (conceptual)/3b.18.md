The line of best fit for this data has a slope of approximately 5. What does this slope represent in the context of the study?

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets the slope and intercept of a regression line in context.

##### Aligned Content
Lines of best fit (conceptual)

##### Explanation
In a linear regression model ($y = mx + b$), the slope ($m$) represents the change in the dependent variable ($y$) for every one-unit increase in the independent variable ($x$). In this context, a slope of 5 means that for every additional hour of homework (the $x$-variable), the predicted exam score (the $y$-variable) increases by 5 points.

##### Domain
Education

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
The predicted decrease in exam score for each additional hour of homework completed — This incorrectly identifies the direction of the relationship. Since the slope is positive (+5), the dependent variable ($y$) is predicted to increase as the independent variable ($x$) increases, not decrease.

###### Option 3
The number of homework hours required to increase an exam score by exactly one point — This describes the reciprocal of the slope ($1/m$). It incorrectly identifies the change in $x$ per unit of $y$ rather than the change in $y$ per unit of $x$.

###### Option 4
The average number of homework hours completed by all students in the study — This describes a univariate measure (the mean of the $x$-variable) rather than the bivariate relationship represented by the slope of the regression line.
