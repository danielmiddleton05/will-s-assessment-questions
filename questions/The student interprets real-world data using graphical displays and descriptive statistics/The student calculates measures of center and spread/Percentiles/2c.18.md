#### Task Description

**Given:** A reading specialist assessed 20 elementary students on their reading fluency. The scores and the number of students earning each score are shown below:

| Fluency Score | Number of Students |
|---|---|

| 80 | 5 |
| 90 | 3 |

What fluency score represents the 75th percentile for this group of students?

##### Explanation
To find the 75th percentile from a frequency table:
1. Calculate the rank position: $i = n \times (p/100) = 20 \times 0.75 = 15$.
2. Build cumulative frequencies from the table:
   - Score 50: cumulative count = 2
   - Score 60: cumulative count = 2 + 4 = 6
   - Score 70: cumulative count = 6 + 6 = 12
   - Score 80: cumulative count = 12 + 5 = 17
   - Score 90: cumulative count = 17 + 3 = 20
3. The 15th value falls within the group of students who scored 80 (positions 13–17).
4. Therefore, the 75th percentile fluency score is 80.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
75 — Interpolation error

The student correctly identified that the 15th position (the 75th percentile rank) falls between the cumulative frequency for a score of 70 (12 students) and a score of 80 (17 students). Instead of identifying the score at the 15th position, the student incorrectly calculated the midpoint between the two score categories (70 and 80).

###### Option 3
85 — Boundary interpolation error

The student may have correctly identified the 80-score range but incorrectly interpolated toward the next score (90), or confused the score with the percentile rank of the final student in that bracket (17/20 = 85th percentile).

###### Option 4
90 — Identification of the maximum

The student selected the highest score in the dataset, confusing the 75th percentile with the maximum value (100th percentile) or incorrectly reasoning that being in the top quartile corresponds to the highest possible score category.
