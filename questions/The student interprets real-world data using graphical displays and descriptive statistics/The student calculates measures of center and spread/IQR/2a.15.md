**Given:** Final exam scores for an education course have the following summary statistics: $Q_1 = 68$, Median = 76, $Q_3 = 85$.

##### Explanation
The interquartile range (IQR) is calculated as $Q_3$ minus $Q_1$: IQR = 85 - 68 = 17. The IQR represents the spread of the middle 50% of the data and is a measure of variability.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Calculates median minus $Q_1$. Students may subtract $Q_1$ from the median (76 - 68 = 8) instead of using $Q_3$ and $Q_1$.

###### Option 2
Calculates $Q_3$ minus median. Students may subtract the median from $Q_3$ (85 - 76 = 9) instead of using the correct formula.

###### Option 4
Calculates $Q_3 + Q_1$. Students may incorrectly add the first and third quartiles (85 + 68 = 153) instead of finding the difference between them.
