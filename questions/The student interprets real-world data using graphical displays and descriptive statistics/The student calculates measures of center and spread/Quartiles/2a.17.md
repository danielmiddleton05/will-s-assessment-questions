#### Task Description

**Given:** Emergency room wait times (in minutes) for 19 patients were recorded and arranged in order from shortest to longest. The 5th value in this ordered list is 12 minutes.

For this dataset of 19 values, the value of 12 minutes represents which quartile?

##### Explanation
For a dataset of 19 values arranged in order, the median (Q2) is the 10th value, which splits the data into two halves of 9 values each. The first quartile (Q1) is defined as the median of the lower half. In a lower half consisting of 9 values, the median is the 5th value ($(9+1)/2 = 5$). Therefore, the 5th value represents Q1.

##### Distractors

###### Option 2
Q2 (median) — Confuses Q1 position with median position.

The median (Q2) for 19 values is the 10th position. This distractor targets those who misidentify the center of the entire dataset.

###### Option 3
Q3 (third quartile) — Confuses quartile positions.

Q3 for 19 values is the median of the upper half (the 15th position). This distractor appeals to those who don't understand the relationship between quartile number and its relative position in the ordered list.

###### Option 4
Maximum — Misidentifies the 5th value as the largest value in the dataset. 

The maximum for 19 values would be at the 19th position. This distractor targets students who confuse the various components of the five-number summary or fail to associate the 5th position with the first quarter of the data.
