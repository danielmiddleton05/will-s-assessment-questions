#### Task Description

A small marketing agency has 7 employees with the following annual salaries (in thousands of dollars): 42, 45, 48, 52, 55, 58, 210. The highest salary belongs to the company owner. Which measure of center would better represent the typical employee salary?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Mean, because it includes all data values.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Mean, because the outlier is a valid data point.


###### Option 3 Correct
Median, because it is not affected by the extreme value.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Median, because it is larger than the mean.

##### Aligned Content
Mean/Median

##### Explanation
The owner's salary of \$210,000 is an extreme outlier that pulls the mean up to about \$72,900—higher than what six of the seven employees actually earn. The median salary of \$52,000 is resistant to this outlier and better represents what a typical employee at this agency makes.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Mean, because it includes all data values — True but not relevant here. While true, this property makes the mean sensitive to the outlier. Including the extreme salary distorts the representation of "typical" earnings.

###### Option 2
Mean, because the outlier is a valid data point — Incorrect principle. While outliers are valid data, they shouldn't dominate a measure of center when the goal is to describe typical values.

###### Option 4
Median, because it is larger than the mean — Incorrect statement. This is factually incorrect for this dataset. In right-skewed distributions like this one, the mean is typically larger than the median. The correct reason to choose median is its resistance to outliers.
