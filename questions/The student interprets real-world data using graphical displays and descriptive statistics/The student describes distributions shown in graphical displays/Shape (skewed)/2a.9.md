##### Explanation
The distribution is skewed right (positively skewed) because the tail extends toward higher values (longer wait times). When data clusters at lower values with a long tail stretching toward higher values, the distribution is right-skewed.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Symmetric — Ignores the tail
Symmetric distributions have equal tails on both sides. Students may select this if they focus only on where most data falls without considering the extended tail.

###### Option 2
Skewed left — Reverses skew direction
Left-skewed distributions have tails extending toward lower values. Students may confuse the direction terminology or misidentify which side contains the tail.

###### Option 3
Uniform — Misidentifies the shape
Uniform distributions have approximately equal frequencies across all values. This targets students unfamiliar with distribution shape terminology.
