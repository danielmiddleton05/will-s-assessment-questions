##### Explanation
A positive association means that as one variable increases, the other variable tends to increase as well. Since recovery time tends to increase as age increases (points move upward from left to right), this represents a positive association between age and recovery time.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Negative association — Reverses the relationship direction
Negative association would mean that as one variable increases, the other decreases. Students may confuse the direction terminology or misinterpret the upward trend.

###### Option 3
No association — Misses the visible pattern
No association would appear as a random scatter with no discernible direction. Students who don't recognize the upward trend or who expect a stronger pattern might select this option.

###### Option 4
Perfect association — Overstates the relationship strength
Perfect association would show all points falling exactly on a line. The description says "generally" move upward, indicating a trend but not perfection. Students may confuse the presence of an association with a perfect linear relationship.
