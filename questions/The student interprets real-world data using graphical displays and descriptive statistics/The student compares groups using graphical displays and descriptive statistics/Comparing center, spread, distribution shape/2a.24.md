##### Explanation
Both distributions are centered at 100 Mbps (similar center). Provider A's range is 30 Mbps (115 - 85), while Provider B's range is 80 Mbps (140 - 60). Provider B has much greater spread while both share the same center.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
This reverses which provider has greater spread. Provider A's narrower range (30 Mbps) indicates less spread compared to Provider B's range (80 Mbps). Students may confuse which provider has the larger range.

###### Option 3
This option incorrectly identifies both measures. Both providers have the same center (100 Mbps), not different centers. Additionally, the spreads are different, not similar. Students may misinterpret the given information about central tendency.

###### Option 4
This option correctly identifies that the spreads differ but incorrectly claims the centers are different. Both distributions are centered at 100 Mbps. Students may focus only on the different ranges and assume all characteristics differ.
