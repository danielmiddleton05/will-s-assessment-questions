##### Explanation
Comparing Year 1 to Year 2 for each age group: Ages 18-24 increased from 5,000 to 8,000 (increase of 3,000). Ages 25-34 increased from 7,000 to 7,500 (increase of 500). Ages 35-44 decreased from 4,000 to 3,500 (decrease of 500). Only the 35-44 age group showed a decrease.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Ages 18-24 — This age group showed the largest absolute change (3,000), which candidates might confuse with a decrease if they focus on magnitude rather than direction of change. This tests whether candidates can distinguish between the size of change and the direction of change.

###### Option 2
Ages 25-34 — This age group showed one of the smallest absolute changes (a 500 increase), which candidates might incorrectly interpret as a decrease if they associate a small margin of growth with a downward trend. This tests understanding that a small increase is still an increase, not a decrease.

###### Option 4
No age group showed a decrease — This option tests whether candidates can accurately identify the direction of change for each category. Candidates might select this if they fail to notice the decrease in the 35-44 age group or if they misread the data, assuming all groups show increases.
