##### Explanation
To determine which department is closest to its storage limit, calculate the remaining capacity for each department by subtracting used storage from allocated storage: Marketing has 50 GB remaining (500 - 450), Engineering has 150 GB remaining (800 - 650), and Sales has 20 GB remaining (400 - 380). Since Sales has the smallest remaining capacity at 20 GB, it is closest to reaching its allocation limit.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Marketing — Selects department with high usage amount rather than least remaining capacity
Marketing has used 450 GB, which is a large amount, but still has 50 GB remaining. Students may focus on the large "Used" bar rather than calculating the gap between allocated and used storage.

###### Option 2
Engineering — Selects department with highest absolute usage
Engineering has the highest used storage (650 GB), which may attract students who confuse highest usage with being closest to the limit. However, Engineering has 150 GB remaining—the most capacity available of all departments.

###### Option 4
Marketing and Sales are equally close to their limits — Misinterprets visual gaps without calculating values
Marketing has a 50 GB gap and Sales has a 20 GB gap. A student may incorrectly assume these small gaps are equivalent compared to Engineering's much larger 150 GB gap, especially when observing the bars visually without performing precise subtraction.
