##### Explanation
To find how many more locations fall in the $100,000–$149,999 range than in the $200,000+ range, read both frequencies from the histogram: the $100,000–$149,999 interval has 24 locations, and the $200,000+ interval has 8 locations. The phrase "how many more" indicates subtraction: 24 − 8 = 16 more locations generated revenue in the $100,000–$149,999 range compared to those generating $200,000 or more.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
This distractor targets students who only read the frequency for the $200,000+ interval (8 locations) without performing the comparison. This reflects incomplete reading of the histogram or misunderstanding that "how many more" requires finding a difference between two values.

###### Option 3
This distractor targets students who only read the frequency for the $100,000–$149,999 interval (24 locations) and provide that single value as the answer. This represents a failure to recognize that the question requires comparing two intervals rather than reporting a single frequency.

###### Option 4
This distractor targets students who add the two frequencies (24 + 8 = 32) instead of finding the difference. This is a common error when students misinterpret "how many more" as requiring addition rather than subtraction, reflecting a misunderstanding of comparative language in data interpretation.
