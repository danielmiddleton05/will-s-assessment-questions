How many more students in the morning section earned an A or B compared to students in the evening section?

##### Explanation
To find how many more morning section students earned an A or B compared to evening section students, locate the "Earned A or B" column and identify the values for both rows: Morning (56) and Evening (38). Then calculate the difference: 56 − 38 = 18 students.

##### Distractors

This targets confusion in identifying the correct related values across different sections and categories.
