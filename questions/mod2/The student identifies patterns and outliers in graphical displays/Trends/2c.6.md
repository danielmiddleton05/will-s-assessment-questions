##### Explanation
Despite daily fluctuations typical of website traffic, the page view values demonstrate a clear upward trajectory from approximately 820 views on Day 1 to approximately 1,420 views by Day 30. This represents an increasing trend, which is characterized by data values that generally rise over the observed time period, even when short-term variations exist between individual data points.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Daily page views remained relatively constant throughout the month — Misidentifies as a horizontal trend.
Students may select this if they focus primarily on the day-to-day fluctuations around local values rather than examining the overall direction from the beginning to the end of the dataset. A horizontal (constant) trend would show values fluctuating around a stable average without consistent directional movement.

###### Option 2
Daily page views exhibited random variation without consistent direction — Denies the presence of a pattern.
Students selecting this may focus exclusively on the natural day-to-day variability in website traffic and fail to recognize that the overall directional change from beginning to end constitutes a trend. They may expect all data points in a trend to follow a perfectly smooth path without any variation.

###### Option 4
Daily page views demonstrated a steady decline throughout the month — Reverses the direction of change.
Students may select this if they misread the scatter plot direction, start their analysis from the wrong end of the time axis, or confuse the concepts of increasing and decreasing. This distractor assesses understanding of basic trend terminology and the ability to correctly interpret directional movement in graphical displays.
