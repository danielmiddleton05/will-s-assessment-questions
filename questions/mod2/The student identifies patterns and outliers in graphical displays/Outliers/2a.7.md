##### Explanation
An outlier is a value significantly different from other observations. The value 15 absences is far removed from the cluster of data between 0-4 absences. There is a large gap between 4 and 15, making 15 a clear outlier.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
0 absences — Confuses an endpoint with an outlier. While 0 is at the low end of the main cluster, it is not separated from other values by a gap. Students may confuse extreme values within a cluster with outliers.

###### Option 2
2 absences — Identifies the mode instead of the outlier. This is the most frequent value (15 students). Students may confuse the concept of outlier with other statistical terms or select a central value.

###### Option 3
4 absences — Confuses the upper boundary of the cluster with an outlier. While 4 is at the higher end of the main cluster, it is not separated by a gap from other values. This targets the distinction between cluster boundaries and true outliers.
