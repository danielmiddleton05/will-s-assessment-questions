##### Explanation
The data shows two distinct phases: growth from \$50,000 to \$120,000 during the first 12 months, followed by decline from \$120,000 to \$45,000 during the next 12 months. This rise-then-fall pattern is the key overall characteristic of the data.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Sales showed consistent growth throughout both years — Ignores the decline
This only describes the first year. The significant decline in year two contradicts the idea of consistent growth throughout.

###### Option 2
Sales decreased steadily over the entire two-year period — Ignores first-year growth
This describes only the general direction from start (\$50,000) to end (\$45,000), missing the important rise during year one.

###### Option 4
Sales showed similar cyclical patterns in both years — Misreads pattern as repeating
This incorrectly interprets the data as showing a repeating annual cycle. The graph actually shows one extended pattern: growth across the entire first year followed by decline across the entire second year, not two separate growth-decline cycles.
