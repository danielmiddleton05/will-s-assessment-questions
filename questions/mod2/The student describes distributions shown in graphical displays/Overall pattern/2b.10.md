#### Internal Notes

##### Domain

##### Evidence Statement
The student describes distributions shown in graphical displays

##### Aligned Content
Overall pattern

##### Explanation
The overall pattern shows clustering of data in the 15-45 minute range, which represents where most patients' wait times fall. The isolated bar at 90-100 minutes, separated by a gap, represents a small group of higher wait times. This description captures both the main concentration of data and the unusual feature.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
The distribution is right-skewed with wait times gradually tapering toward higher values — Misinterprets the gap as gradual tapering. While right-skewed distributions have a tail extending toward higher values, this histogram shows a clear gap between 50-90 minutes rather than a gradual decrease. Students who recognize the higher values but don't notice the gap may select this option.

###### Option 2
The distribution is symmetric with data evenly balanced around 50 minutes — Misidentifies center and shape. The distribution is not symmetric; it clusters at lower values with an isolated group of higher values. Students might incorrectly assume symmetry or misinterpret the midpoint of the range as the center of the distribution.

###### Option 4
The distribution shows no discernible pattern among the wait time data — Ignores clear clustering. The clustering of data in a specific range is itself a clear pattern. Students who don't recognize clustering as a meaningful pattern in statistical distributions might select this option.
