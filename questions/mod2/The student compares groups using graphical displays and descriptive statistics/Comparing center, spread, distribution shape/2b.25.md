#### Task Description

**Given:** A business analyst compared customer satisfaction scores for two store locations. Store A had a mean of 72, a median of 71, and a standard deviation of 12; Store B had a mean of 72, a median of 71, and a standard deviation of 6.

How do the two distributions compare in terms of center and spread?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Store B has a higher center and greater spread than Store A.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Both stores have the same center but Store B has greater spread.


###### Option 3 Correct
Both stores have the same center but Store A has greater spread.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Store A has a higher center and greater spread than Store B.

##### Explanation
Both stores have the same mean (72) and the same median (71), indicating equal centers regardless of which measure is used. However, Store A's standard deviation (12) is twice that of Store B (6), indicating Store A has greater spread in satisfaction scores.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Store B has a higher center and greater spread than Store A — Reverses both comparisons. Students might reverse both comparisons, thinking the smaller standard deviation indicates more spread or misreading which store has which values.

###### Option 2
Both stores have the same center but Store B has greater spread — Reverses the spread comparison. Students might correctly identify that both stores have identical centers but reverse which store has greater spread, either by confusing which standard deviation belongs to which store or by incorrectly believing that a smaller standard deviation indicates more variability.

###### Option 4
Store A has a higher center and greater spread than Store B — Incorrectly claims different centers. Both stores have identical centers (mean = 72, median = 71 for both), so this option is incorrect about center while correct about spread. Students might misread the given statistics, swapping which values belong to which store, or incorrectly believe that greater spread implies a higher center.
