##### Explanation
Protocol A: median = 11 days, IQR = Q3 - Q1 = 14 - 8 = 6 days. Protocol B: median = 15 days, IQR = Q3 - Q1 = 21 - 9 = 12 days. Protocol A has both the lower median (11 < 15) and the smaller IQR (6 < 12).

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Reverses both comparisons. Protocol B has a higher median (15 vs 11) and larger IQR (12 vs 6). Students may misread the box plot values or confuse which protocol has which statistics.

###### Option 2
Correctly identifies that Protocol A has the lower median, but incorrectly claims Protocol B has the smaller IQR. Protocol A actually has the smaller IQR (6 days vs 12 days). This distractor appeals to students who correctly calculate the median but make an error when calculating or comparing the IQR values.

###### Option 3
Incorrectly claims Protocol B has the lower median, though it correctly identifies that Protocol A has the smaller IQR. Protocol B has the higher median (15 vs 11). This distractor appeals to students who correctly calculate IQR but reverse the median comparison or confuse which protocol has which median value.
