##### Aligned Content
Comparing center, spread, distribution shape

##### Explanation
Consistency in data is measured by spread—lower spread indicates more consistent values. Server B has a standard deviation of 3, compared to Server A's standard deviation of 8. The lower standard deviation means Server B's response times vary less from the mean, indicating more consistent performance despite having a higher average response time.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Server A confuses lower mean with consistency. Students might think that Server A's lower mean (45 vs 52) indicates better or more consistent performance, incorrectly associating a lower center value with reduced variability.

###### Option 2
Both servers equally ignores the standard deviation comparison. This option may be selected by students who do not recognize that standard deviation directly measures consistency and variability, or who believe the distributions are comparable without analyzing spread.

###### Option 3
Cannot be determined with this information reflects overly cautious reasoning. Standard deviation is a direct and sufficient measure of variability and consistency. Students selecting this option may not understand that the provided statistics are adequate for comparing consistency between the two servers.
