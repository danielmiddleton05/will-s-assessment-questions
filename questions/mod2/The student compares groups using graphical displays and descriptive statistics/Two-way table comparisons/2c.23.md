#### Task Description

**Given:** A technology company surveyed customers about their preferred device type for completing work tasks.

| Device Type | Remote Workers | Office Workers | Total |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|-------|
| Laptop      | 34             | 68             | 102   |
| Desktop     | 27             | 74             | 101   |
| Tablet      | 64             | 47             | 111   |
| Smartphone  | 45             | 41             | 86    |
| Total       | 170            | 230            | 400   |

Which device group is used more overall: compact devices (Tablet and Smartphone) or big-screen devices (Laptop and Desktop)?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Correct
Big-screen devices (Laptop and Desktop)


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Compact devices (Tablet and Smartphone)


###### Option 3 Incorrect
Both groups are used equally


###### Option 4 Incorrect
This cannot be determined from the data, because big-screen and compact device categories are not present in the table

##### Explanation
To determine which device group is used more overall, calculate the sum of the totals for each category. For Desktop devices: Laptop (102) + Desktop (101) = 203. For Compact devices: Tablet (111) + Smartphone (86) = 197. Since 203 is greater than 197, desktop devices are used more overall.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Tablet has the highest individual total of any device (111). Students may select this option if they focus on the most popular single category rather than summing the combined totals for the group. Additionally, the totals are very close (203 vs. 197), which may lead to errors if the addition is performed incorrectly.

###### Option 3
The totals for the two groups (203 and 197) are close, representing 50.75% and 49.25% of the data respectively. Students who estimate the values or use rounding might conclude that there is no significant difference and that the groups are used equally.

###### Option 4
This distractor targets students who misunderstand the concept of two-way tables being summaries of data that are meant to be manipulated
