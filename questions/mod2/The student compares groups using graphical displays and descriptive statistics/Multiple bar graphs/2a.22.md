##### Explanation
Calculating the differences for each quarter: Q1: $85 - 78 = 7$, Q2: $92 - 88 = 4$, Q3: $105 - 95 = 10$, Q4: $118 - 109 = 9$. Q3 has the greatest sales difference at 10 thousand dollars, making it the correct answer.
