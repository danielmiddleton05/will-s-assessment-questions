##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Surgery — Selects smallest improvement
Surgery improved by only 0.1, the smallest improvement. Students may misread the graph or miscalculate differences.

###### Option 3
Pediatrics — Selects highest final value instead of greatest change
Pediatrics has the highest Year 2 rating (4.5) but not the greatest improvement (0.3 vs Emergency's 1.0). Students may confuse highest value with greatest change.

###### Option 4
All units (equal improvement) — Doesn't calculate the differences
The improvements are 1.0, 0.1, and 0.3 respectively—clearly unequal. This targets students who note that all improved without calculating magnitudes.
