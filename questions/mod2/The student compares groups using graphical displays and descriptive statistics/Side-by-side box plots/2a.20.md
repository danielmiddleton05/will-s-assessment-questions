##### Explanation
The median is represented by the middle value in a box plot's five-number summary. Model X has a median of 10 hours, while Model Y has a median of 9 hours. Therefore, Model X has the higher median battery life by 1 hour. The median is a measure of central tendency and is independent of the range or maximum values of the dataset.
