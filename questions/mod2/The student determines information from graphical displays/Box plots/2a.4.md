##### Explanation
The median is represented by the line inside the box of a box plot. In this case, the median is 2.8 seconds. The minimum (1.2) is at the left whisker, Q1 (2.1) marks the left edge of the box, Q3 (3.5) marks the right edge of the box, and the maximum (4.2) is at the right whisker.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1

###### Option 2
