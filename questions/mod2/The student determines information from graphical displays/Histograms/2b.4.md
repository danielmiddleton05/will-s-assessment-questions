##### Explanation
To find the number of patients who waited less than 30 minutes, add the frequencies for the two bins that fall below 30 minutes: the 0-15 minute bin (45 patients) and the 15-30 minute bin (82 patients). The calculation is 45 + 82 = 127 patients.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Represents only the 0-15 minute bin. This targets students who interpret "less than 30 minutes" as only the first interval, failing to recognize that the 15-30 minute range also qualifies.

###### Option 2
Represents only the 15-30 minute bin. This targets students who focus on the bin ending at 30 minutes while forgetting to include the 0-15 minute bin in their calculation.

###### Option 4
Represents the sum of three bins (45 + 82 + 67 = 194). This targets students who incorrectly interpret "less than 30 minutes" to include the 30-45 minute bin, misunderstanding that this bin represents patients who waited 30 minutes or more.
