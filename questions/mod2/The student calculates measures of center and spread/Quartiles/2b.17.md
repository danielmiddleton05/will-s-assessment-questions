##### Explanation
For 8 values, Q1 is the median of the lower half (45, 53, 58, 62). The median of these four values is $\frac{53 + 58}{2} = 55.5$. Q1 represents the 25th percentile, where 25% of the data falls below this value.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
54.5 — Calculates the mean of the lower half. Students might calculate the average of the four values in the lower half ($\frac{45+53+58+62}{4}$) instead of finding the median.

###### Option 3
64.5 — Calculates the median (Q2) of the entire dataset. Students might calculate the median of the full data set (average of 62 and 67) instead of the lower half.

###### Option 4
74.5 — Calculates the third quartile (Q3). Students might identify the median of the upper half (average of 71 and 78) instead of the lower half.
