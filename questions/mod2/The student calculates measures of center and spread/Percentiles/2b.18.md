##### Explanation
To calculate the 80th percentile of a dataset:
1. Sort the data from least to greatest: \$110,000, \$125,000, \$140,000, \$165,000, \$180,000.
2. Determine the rank ($i$) of the percentile by multiplying the total number of values ($n$) by the percentile ($p$): $i = n \times (p/100)$.
3. Calculation: $5 \times 0.80 = 4$.
4. The 80th percentile is the 4th value in the sorted list, which is \$165,000.
