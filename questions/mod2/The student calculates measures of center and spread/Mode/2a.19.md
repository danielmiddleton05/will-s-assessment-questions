##### Explanation
The mode is the value that appears most frequently in a data set. Counting each tip amount: \$2 appears 1 time, \$3 appears 4 times, \$4 appears 2 times, \$5 appears 2 times, and \$6 appears 1 time. Since \$3 occurs more often than any other value, the mode is \$3.00.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
\$3.50 — Calculates the median instead of the mode. When the data is ordered (2, 3, 3, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6), the median is the average of the 5th and 6th values: (3 + 4) ÷ 2 = 3.50. This targets candidates who confuse mode with median or who find the middle value instead of the most frequent value.

###### Option 3
\$3.80 — Calculates the mean instead of the mode. This is the arithmetic mean (2 + 3 + 5 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 6 + 5 + 3 + 4 = 38; 38 ÷ 10 = 3.80). This targets candidates who confuse mode with mean or who default to calculating an average when asked for a measure of center.

###### Option 4
\$4.00 — Selects the second most frequent value. The value \$4 appears 2 times, tied with \$5 for second-most frequent. This targets candidates who may have miscounted frequencies, selected the middle of the range of distinct values, or confused which value had the highest count.
