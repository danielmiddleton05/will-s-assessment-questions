#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital recorded the wait times (in minutes) for 7 emergency room patients on a particular evening: 12, 18, 22, 25, 28, 35, 120.

What are the mean and median wait times for these patients?

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student interprets real-world data using graphical displays and descriptive statistics.

##### Evidence Statement
The student calculates measures of center and spread.

##### Aligned Content
Mean/Median

##### Explanation
To find the mean, sum all wait times and divide by the total number of patients: (12 + 18 + 22 + 25 + 28 + 35 + 120) / 7 = 260 / 7 ≈ 37.1 minutes. 
To find the median, identify the middle value in an ordered data set. With 7 values already arranged from smallest to largest, the middle position is the 4th value. Counting from the left: 12 (1st), 18 (2nd), 22 (3rd), 25 (4th). The median wait time is 25 minutes.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Calculates the mean correctly but selects the 3rd value (22) instead of the 4th for the median. This indicates a misunderstanding of how to locate the center position in an odd-numbered data set.

###### Option 3
Calculates the mean correctly but selects the 5th value (28) instead of the 4th for the median. This suggests an off-by-one error in determining the median's index within the ordered list.

###### Option 4
Identifies the median correctly but applies that value to both the mean and the median. This targets students who confuse the two definitions or incorrectly assume that the mean and median are always identical measures of center.
