#### Task Description

**Given:** A teacher records quiz scores for 7 students: 68, 72, 75, 75, 78, 82, 95.

What is the median score for this class?

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student interprets real-world data using graphical displays and descriptive statistics.

##### Evidence Statement
The student calculates measures of center and spread

##### Aligned Content
Mean/Median

##### Explanation
With 7 values already in order, the median is the middle (4th) value. Counting to the 4th position: 68, 72, 75, **75**, 78, 82, 95. The median is 75.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
76.5 — Averages the two middle values (75 and 78) unnecessarily. This would be the approach if there were an even number of values. With 7 values (odd), the median is simply the middle value.

###### Option 3
77.86 — Calculates the mean instead. The sum is 545 and 545 ÷ 7 ≈ 77.86. This targets candidates who confuse median with mean or calculate the wrong measure of center.

###### Option 4
78 — Counts to wrong position. This is the 5th value, not the 4th. Candidates may miscount positions when finding the middle value.
