##### Explanation
Annual income is numerical because it represents a measurable quantity of money. Marital status, gender, and vehicle color are categorical variables; they describe qualitative characteristics and place individuals into distinct groups rather than representing a measurement or count.

##### Domain
Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Marital status — Confuses categorical data with numerical
Marital status describes a qualitative characteristic, placing individuals into groups such as single, married, or divorced.

###### Option 2
Gender — Confuses categorical data with numerical
Gender is a qualitative variable that places individuals into groups.

###### Option 4
Vehicle color — Confuses categorical data with numerical
Vehicle color describes a characteristic or quality, making it a categorical variable.
