##### Explanation
Payment method (cash, credit, debit) is categorical because it represents distinct groups or categories without inherent numerical meaning. Purchase amount and transaction time are continuous numerical variables measured in quantities, and number of items is a discrete numerical variable representing counts.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Purchase amount in dollars — Confuses measured quantities with categories
Students may select this if they don't recognize that dollar amounts are numerical values that can be ordered, averaged, and calculated. This targets those who confuse "has numbers in it" with "is categorical."

###### Option 3
Number of items bought — Mistakes count data for categorical
This distractor targets students who confuse discrete numerical data (counts) with categorical data. While both involve distinct values, counts represent quantities that can be meaningfully ordered and averaged.

###### Option 4
Total transaction time in minutes — Confuses continuous measurement with categories
Students selecting this may not distinguish between data that represents measurable quantities on a continuous scale versus data that represents group membership without numerical meaning.
