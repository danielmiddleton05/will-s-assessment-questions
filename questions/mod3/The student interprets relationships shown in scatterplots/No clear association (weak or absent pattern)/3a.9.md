#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets relationships shown in scatterplots.

##### Aligned Content
No clear association (weak or absent pattern)

##### Explanation
When data points are randomly scattered with no discernible pattern, this indicates no clear association between the variables. A strong positive relationship would show points clustering around an upward trend. A moderate negative relationship would show points clustering around a downward trend. The visual pattern of random scatter is sufficient to conclude no association exists without requiring calculations.

##### Domain
Health

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
There is a weak positive relationship between shoe size and blood pressure. — Misidentifies random scatter as a weak positive trend
By framing the relationship as weak, this distractor is more plausible than a strong relationship, requiring students to carefully examine the scatterplot for any subtle upward trend rather than dismissing the option based solely on real-world intuition.

###### Option 3
There is a moderate negative relationship between shoe size and blood pressure. — Misidentifies random scatter as negative trend
A negative relationship requires data points to follow a downward pattern where increases in one variable correspond to decreases in the other. This distractor targets students who might arbitrarily assign a direction to data that shows no actual pattern, failing to recognize that absence of trend means absence of relationship.

###### Option 4
The relationship cannot be determined without calculating the correlation coefficient. — Overreliance on numerical calculation
While correlation coefficients quantify the strength and direction of relationships, the visual pattern of completely random scatter provides sufficient evidence to conclude no association exists. This distractor targets students who believe statistical calculations are always required, not recognizing that a scatterplot showing random scatter clearly communicates the absence of relationship.
