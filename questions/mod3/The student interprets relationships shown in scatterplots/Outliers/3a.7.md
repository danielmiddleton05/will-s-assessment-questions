Outliers

##### Explanation
An outlier is a data point that lies far from the general pattern of the other data. This student's combination of high attendance with an unusually low score deviates substantially from where other similar students fall, making it an outlier.

##### Domain

Education

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
A trend describes the overall direction or pattern in data across many points, not the characteristic of an individual observation.

###### Option 3
A cluster refers to a grouping of multiple data points that are close together, not a single unusual point separated from others.

###### Option 4
A representative point would fall within the typical pattern of the data. This point is specifically described as far from students with similar attendance.
