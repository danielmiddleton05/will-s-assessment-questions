#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets relationships shown in scatterplots.

##### Aligned Content
Form (linear, nonlinear)

##### Explanation
A linear relationship is characterized by points following a straight-line pattern where the rate of change remains constant. The description indicates that material costs increase at a consistent rate with each unit produced, and the points cluster around a straight trend line.

##### Domain
Business

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Nonlinear — Misidentifies a straight pattern as curved
Students may select this if they overthink or are unsure what "linear" means. The constant rate of change explicitly described indicates linearity, not curvature.

###### Option 3
Exponential — Introduces a specific nonlinear form not described
Exponential growth shows accelerating increases, not constant rates. This distractor appeals to students who recognize exponential as a term from math but misapply it to any increasing relationship.

###### Option 4
No clear form — Ignores the described pattern
The question explicitly states points cluster around a straight trend, directly contradicting "no clear form." This catches students who dismiss patterns they find unfamiliar.
