##### Explanation
The relationship shows a curved pattern—productivity first rises and then falls as hours increase. This inverted-U shape cannot be captured by a straight line, making it nonlinear. A linear relationship would show a consistent increase or decrease throughout, whereas this trend reverses direction.

##### Domain

Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
An exponential relationship involves a rate of change that strictly increases or decreases (monotonic), failing to account for the peak and subsequent decline in productivity.

###### Option 3
A periodic relationship would involve a repeating pattern over regular intervals, whereas this trend shows a single peak and decline within the observed range.

###### Option 4
There is a clear, describable pattern; it simply follows a curve rather than a straight line.
