#### Internal Notes

##### Competency

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets relationships shown in scatterplots.

##### Aligned Content
Clusters, patterns, deviations

##### Explanation
Clusters are distinct groupings of data points that are separated from each other by gaps or space. Two separate groupings suggest there may be meaningful subgroups in the data, perhaps representing different server configurations or usage periods.

##### Domain

Technology

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Random scatter would show no discernible pattern or grouping. The presence of two distinct groupings indicates a clear pattern, not randomness. This distractor appeals to candidates who fail to recognize the structured pattern in the data.

###### Option 2
Linear trend would show points following a single line pattern, not separated into distinct groups with a gap between them. This distractor appeals to candidates who confuse any observable pattern with linear relationships.

###### Option 4
Single outlier is one extreme point, not two distinct groups of points. The description indicates multiple points in each grouping. This distractor appeals to candidates who confuse grouped deviations with individual anomalies.
