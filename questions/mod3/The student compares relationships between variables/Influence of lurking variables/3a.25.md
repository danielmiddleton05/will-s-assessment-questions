#### Task Description

**Given:** A retail analysis reveals a strong positive correlation between the number of salespeople working and daily sales revenue. A store manager concludes that simply hiring more salespeople will directly increase revenue. What potential lurking variable could make this conclusion questionable?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Cash register brand, which may affect transaction processing efficiency.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Schedule arrangement method, which may determine shift coverage patterns.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
Inventory ordering schedule, which may determine product availability timing.


###### Option 4 Correct
Customer traffic volume, which may drive both staffing decisions and sales totals.

##### Explanation
A lurking variable influences both the independent and dependent variables, creating a correlation without direct causation. Customer traffic could lead managers to schedule more staff on busy days, and more customers also means more potential sales—explaining the correlation without staffing directly causing sales.

##### Domain

Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
The brand of cash registers — This is unlikely to vary systematically with staffing levels or to directly influence sales volume in a way that creates the observed correlation.

###### Option 2
The schedule arrangement method — While it may determine shift coverage patterns, this administrative detail would not plausibly influence both daily staffing levels and sales revenue to create the observed correlation.

###### Option 3
The dates when inventory was ordered — While inventory matters for sales, ordering dates would not create the observed correlation between daily staffing and daily sales.
