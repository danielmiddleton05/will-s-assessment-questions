#### Task Description

**Given:** A data scientist compares two linear regression models derived from different datasets:

- **Model A:** $\hat{y} = 5.5 + 3.2x$ (The correlation coefficient is $r = 0.67$).
- **Model B:** $\hat{y} = 12.0 - 0.5x$ (The correlation coefficient is $r = -0.91$).

Which statement accurately compares the direction and strength of these two relationships?

##### Explanation
**Direction:** Determined by the sign of the correlation coefficient ($r$) or the slope coefficient in the regression equation ($\hat{y} = mx + b$).
- Model A has $r = 0.67$ and a slope of $+3.2$, indicating a **positive** relationship.
- Model B has $r = -0.91$ and a slope of $-0.5$, indicating a **negative** relationship.

**Strength:** Determined by the magnitude (absolute value) of the correlation coefficient ($|r|$). The closer the value is to 1 or -1, the stronger the relationship.
- Model A: $|0.67| = 0.67$.
- Model B: $|-0.91| = 0.91$.
- Since $0.91 > 0.67$, Model B is **stronger**.

Therefore, Model A is positive and weaker, while Model B is negative and stronger.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Model A represents a stronger relationship because the slope (3.2) is steeper than Model B's slope (-0.5) — This distractor targets the common misconception that a steeper slope implies a stronger correlation. Strength is measured by the magnitude of the correlation coefficient ($|r|$), not by the rate of change (slope).

###### Option 3
Model A is a positive, stronger relationship; Model B is a negative, weaker relationship — This distractor appeals to students who correctly identify the directions but confuse the strength metrics, either by looking at the slope magnitude or failing to take the absolute value of $r$.

###### Option 4
Model A is a negative, weaker relationship; Model B is a positive, stronger relationship — This distractor reverses the directions of the relationships, appealing to students who may be confused about reading the signs of the slope or the correlation coefficient.
