#### Task Description

**Given:** A business analyst examines two relationships: advertising spending and sales revenue show a correlation of $r = 0.78$, while employee training hours and sales revenue show a correlation of $r = 0.41$. What can be concluded from comparing these correlations?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Both variables have equally strong associations with sales revenue.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Training hours has a stronger linear association with sales revenue than advertising spending does.


###### Option 3 Correct
Advertising spending has a stronger linear association with sales revenue than training hours does.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Advertising spending has a negative linear association with sales revenue, while training hours has a positive linear association.

##### Explanation
The absolute value of the correlation coefficient indicates the strength of a linear association. Since $0.78 > 0.41$, advertising spending has a stronger linear association with sales revenue than training hours does.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Both variables have equally strong associations with sales revenue — The correlations differ by 0.37, which represents a meaningful difference in association strength. Students who do not understand how to compare correlation coefficients might incorrectly conclude that both show associations without recognizing the difference in strength.

###### Option 2
Training hours has a stronger linear association with sales revenue than advertising spending does — This reverses the correct comparison. Since $0.41 < 0.78$, training hours has the weaker association. Students who misread or misinterpret the numerical values might select this option.

###### Option 4
Advertising spending has a negative linear association with sales revenue, while training hours has a positive linear association — Both correlation coefficients ($0.78$ and $0.41$) are positive, indicating that both variables have a positive linear relationship with sales revenue. This distractor tests the student's ability to interpret the sign of the correlation coefficient.
