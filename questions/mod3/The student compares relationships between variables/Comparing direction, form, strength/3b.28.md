How do these two relationships compare in terms of direction, form, and strength?

#### Quiz Options

Both relationships are nonlinear; Study A increases and levels off while Study B also increases and levels off, and Study A is stronger.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Both relationships are nonlinear; Study A decreases and levels off while Study B increases then decreases, and Study A is stronger.


###### Option 3 Correct
Both relationships are nonlinear; Study A increases and levels off with a stronger association, while Study B increases then decreases with a weaker association.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Both relationships are nonlinear and similarly strong; Study A increases and levels off while Study B increases then decreases.

##### Explanation
In Study A, the median score change rises quickly at first and then increases by smaller and smaller amounts, indicating a **nonlinear increasing relationship that levels off**. The typical ranges (error bars) are narrow and move upward consistently, indicating a **stronger** association (outcomes track the pattern more consistently).

In Study B, the median score change increases up to 4–5 hours and then declines for higher hours, indicating a **nonlinear relationship with direction that changes (increases then decreases)**. The typical ranges are very wide and overlap heavily across groups, indicating a **weaker** association.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Misidentifies the direction in Study B by treating it as increasing and leveling off (monotonic), ignoring that the median rises and then falls.

###### Option 2
Misidentifies the direction in Study A by describing it as decreasing, even though the medians clearly increase across groups.

###### Option 4
Correctly identifies the nonlinear forms and the directional patterns, but misjudges strength by claiming both are similarly strong; Study B shows much larger within-group variability, indicating a weaker relationship.

```python
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import numpy as np

def setup_style():
    """Configure matplotlib style to match scientific plotting standards."""
    plt.rcParams['font.family'] = 'DejaVu Sans'
    plt.rcParams['font.size'] = 10
    plt.rcParams['axes.titlesize'] = 14
    plt.rcParams['axes.titleweight'] = 'bold'
    plt.rcParams['axes.labelsize'] = 12
    plt.rcParams['axes.labelcolor'] = '#333333'
    plt.rcParams['xtick.labelsize'] = 10
    plt.rcParams['ytick.labelsize'] = 10
    plt.rcParams['xtick.color'] = '#333333'
    plt.rcParams['ytick.color'] = '#333333'
    plt.rcParams['axes.edgecolor'] = '#333333'
    plt.rcParams['axes.linewidth'] = 1.0
    plt.rcParams['grid.color'] = '#CCCCCC'
    plt.rcParams['grid.linewidth'] = 0.5
    plt.rcParams['grid.alpha'] = 1.0
    plt.rcParams['figure.facecolor'] = 'white'
    plt.rcParams['axes.facecolor'] = 'white'
    # Ensure grid is behind data
    plt.rcParams['axes.axisbelow'] = True

def plot_image():
    """Generate the scientific visualization."""
    setup_style()

    # Create figure with 2 side-by-side subplots
    fig, (ax1, ax2) = plt.subplots(1, 2, figsize=(10.24, 6))

    # --- Data Preparation ---
    
    # Study A Data: SkillBuilder Practice → Change in Score
    # Midpoints of ranges: 0-15(7.5), 16-30(23), 31-45(38), 46-60(53), 61+(68 approx)
    x_a = np.array([7.5, 23, 38, 53, 68])
    y_a = np.array([1, 7, 11, 13, 14])
    y_a_min = np.array([-1, 5, 9, 11, 12])
    y_a_max = np.array([3, 9, 13, 15, 16])
    
    # Calculate asymmetric errors for Study A (lower, upper)
    yerr_a = [y_a - y_a_min, y_a_max - y_a]

    # Study B Data: Extracurricular Time → Change in Score
    # Inverted-U shape with wide error bars (weaker association)
    # Midpoints: 0-1(0.5), 2-3(2.5), 4-5(4.5), 6-7(6.5), 8+(9 approx)
    x_b = np.array([0.5, 2.5, 4.5, 6.5, 9])
    y_b = np.array([3, 7, 9, 6, 2])
    y_b_min = np.array([-5, -1, 0, -4, -8])
    y_b_max = np.array([11, 15, 18, 16, 12])
    
    # Calculate asymmetric errors for Study B
    yerr_b = [y_b - y_b_min, y_b_max - y_b]

    # --- Plotting Study A ---
    
    ax1.errorbar(x_a, y_a, yerr=yerr_a, fmt='o', color='#1f77b4', 
                 ecolor='black', elinewidth=1.5, capsize=4, 
                 markeredgecolor='black', markeredgewidth=1, markersize=8)
    
    ax1.set_title("Study A: SkillBuilder Practice", pad=15)
    ax1.set_xlabel("Weekly Minutes")
    ax1.set_ylabel("Change in Score (points)")
    
    ax1.set_xlim(0, 80)
    ax1.set_ylim(-2, 18)
    
    # Horizontal grid only
    ax1.grid(True, axis='y', linestyle='-')
    
    # Hide top and right spines
    ax1.spines['top'].set_visible(False)
    ax1.spines['right'].set_visible(False)

    # --- Plotting Study B ---
    
    ax2.errorbar(x_b, y_b, yerr=yerr_b, fmt='o', color='#1f77b4', 
                 ecolor='black', elinewidth=1.5, capsize=4, 
                 markeredgecolor='black', markeredgewidth=1, markersize=8)
    
    ax2.set_title("Study B: Extracurricular Time", pad=15)
    ax2.set_xlabel("Weekly Hours")
    ax2.set_ylabel("Change in Score (points)")
    
    ax2.set_xlim(0, 10)
    ax2.set_ylim(-10, 20)
    
    # Horizontal grid only
    ax2.grid(True, axis='y', linestyle='-')
    
    # Hide top and right spines
    ax2.spines['top'].set_visible(False)
    ax2.spines['right'].set_visible(False)

    # Adjust layout
    plt.tight_layout(pad=3.0)
    
    return fig

if __name__ == "__main__":
    fig = plot_image()
    plt.savefig('plot.png', dpi=150, bbox_inches='tight')
    print("Image saved as plot.png")
```
