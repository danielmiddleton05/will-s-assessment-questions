#### Task Description

**Given:** A researcher examines how temperature affects daily beverage sales at two types of vendors. The findings are summarized below.

- **Coffee Vendor:** For every 10°F increase in temperature, daily sales decrease by approximately 15 cups.
- **Smoothie Vendor:** For every 10°F increase in temperature, daily sales increase by approximately 25 cups.

What does this comparison suggest about the relationship between temperature and sales?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
Temperature has no meaningful impact on beverage sales for either vendor.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Both vendors experience increased sales as temperature rises.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The coffee vendor sells more beverages overall than the smoothie vendor.


###### Option 4 Correct
Temperature and sales have opposite relationships for the two vendors.

##### Explanation
The coffee vendor shows a negative relationship between temperature and sales—as temperature increases, sales decrease. The smoothie vendor shows a positive relationship—as temperature increases, sales increase. This comparison demonstrates that the same predictor variable (temperature) can have different directional effects on the outcome variable (sales) depending on the group being analyzed.
