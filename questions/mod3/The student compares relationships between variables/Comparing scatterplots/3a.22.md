##### Explanation
The tightness of clustering around a trend indicates association strength. Scatterplot B's tight clustering indicates a stronger relationship than Scatterplot A's wide scatter. Both show negative associations (downward trends), but B's is stronger.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Wide scatter around a trend indicates a weaker association. Tight clustering, as in Scatterplot B, indicates greater strength. This reverses the correct relationship between the two scatterplots.

###### Option 2
The descriptions specifically contrast "scattered widely" with "clustered tightly," indicating different strengths of association. The degree of clustering directly relates to association strength, so these cannot be equal.

###### Option 3
A downward trend indicates a negative association, not a positive one. As exercise frequency increases, resting heart rate decreases, which represents a negative relationship between the variables.
