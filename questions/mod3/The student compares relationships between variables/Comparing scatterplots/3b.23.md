##### Explanation
Both scatterplots show downward trends (upper left to lower right), indicating negative associations. Points in a "tight band" indicate stronger association than "loosely scattered" points. Therefore, Scatterplot X shows a strong negative association, and Scatterplot Y shows a weak negative association.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
This distractor reverses both the direction and strength of the associations. It incorrectly identifies downward trends as positive associations and misinterprets tight clustering as weak and loose scattering as strong. This targets students who confuse the concepts of direction and strength in correlations.

###### Option 2
This distractor incorrectly interprets the tight clustering in X as indicating no association and misidentifies the loose scatter in Y as a strong association rather than a weak one. It targets students who confuse the indicators for association strength, mistakenly thinking a clear pattern means no relationship while a loose pattern indicates a strong one.

###### Option 3
This distractor incorrectly identifies Scatterplot X's downward trend as a positive association while correctly identifying Y as negative. This targets students who misinterpret the direction of slopes, confusing upper-left to lower-right patterns with positive relationships.
