##### Explanation
The explanatory variable is the factor believed to influence or predict changes in another variable. Here, the manager is investigating whether staffing levels (number of sales associates) affect the outcome (sales revenue). The explanatory variable is what the researcher considers the potential cause or predictor.

##### Domain

Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Type of products sold — This variable is not part of the stated research question. Students selecting this may be introducing extraneous factors rather than identifying the variables explicitly described in the study design.

###### Option 3
Total daily sales revenue — This is the response variable, the outcome the manager is trying to explain or predict. Students who select this may be confusing which variable is being measured as the result versus which is being used as the predictor.

###### Option 4
Time of year — While seasonality could influence sales, it is not mentioned as a variable in this study. Students may select this if they are thinking of potential confounding factors rather than focusing on the stated research question.
