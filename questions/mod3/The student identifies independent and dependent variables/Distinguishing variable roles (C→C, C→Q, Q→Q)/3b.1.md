#### Task Description

**Given:** A retail analyst is investigating whether the daily outdoor temperature influences the total sales of hot beverages at a local café. The analyst collects data over 30 days, recording the average daily temperature in degrees Fahrenheit and the total number of hot beverages sold each day.

In this study, which factor is the **response variable**?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
The average daily temperature


###### Option 2 Correct
The total sales of beverages


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The total days of data recorded


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The average price of beverages

##### Competency

##### Explanation
The response variable (also known as the dependent variable) is the outcome being measured or predicted in a study. In this scenario, the analyst is looking at how sales change as a result of temperature. Therefore, the total sales of beverages is the response variable, while the temperature is the explanatory (independent) variable.

##### Domain
Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
The average daily temperature — This is the explanatory or independent variable. It is the factor that is expected to cause a change in the outcome. Students choosing this often reverse the relationship between the cause and the effect.

###### Option 3
The total days of data recorded — This represents the sample size (n = 30). It is a part of the study design and structure, but it is not a variable being measured for its relationship to another factor. Students selecting this may confuse the framework of data collection with the variables within it.

###### Option 4
The average price of beverages — This is a potential confounding or control variable. While price could certainly affect sales, it is not the focus of this specific research question, which is isolated to the impact of temperature. Students may select this if they identify it as a relevant factor in a real-world business context but fail to focus on the specific variables defined in the prompt.
