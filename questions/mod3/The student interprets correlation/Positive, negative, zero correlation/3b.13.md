##### Explanation

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
A zero correlation would mean no consistent pattern between study hours and scores. The described pattern of both increasing together indicates a non-zero correlation. This distractor assesses whether the candidate recognizes when variables have a relationship versus when they do not.

###### Option 3
A negative correlation would mean that as study hours increase, exam scores decrease. This is the opposite of the relationship described. This distractor tests whether the candidate understands directional relationships between variables.

###### Option 4
A perfect correlation would require every increase in study hours to produce an exact, predictable increase in exam scores with no variation. The scenario describes a general tendency, not an exact mathematical relationship. This distractor tests whether the candidate distinguishes between the direction of a correlation and its strength.
