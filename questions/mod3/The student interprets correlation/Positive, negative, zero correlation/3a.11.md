#### Task Description

**Given:** A business analyst calculates the Pearson correlation coefficient between customer complaints and customer retention rate, finding $r = -0.72$.

What does this correlation indicate about the direction of the relationship?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
As complaints increase, retention tends to increase.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Complaints and retention are unrelated.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The relationship changes direction at different complaint levels.


###### Option 4 Correct
As complaints increase, retention tends to decrease.

##### Explanation
A negative correlation means the variables move in opposite directions. When one increases, the other tends to decrease. With $r = -0.72$, higher complaint numbers are associated with lower retention rates.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
As complaints increase, retention tends to increase — Reverses the direction
This describes a positive correlation, not a negative one. Students who confuse the meaning of the negative sign may select this option.

###### Option 2
Complaints and retention are unrelated — Ignores the substantial correlation
A correlation of -0.72 is moderately strong, indicating a meaningful relationship. Students may select this if they associate negative signs with absence rather than direction.

###### Option 3
The relationship changes direction at different complaint levels — Overcomplicates the interpretation
A single correlation coefficient summarizes the overall linear relationship. It does not indicate changing directions; that would require examining the scatterplot for nonlinearity.
