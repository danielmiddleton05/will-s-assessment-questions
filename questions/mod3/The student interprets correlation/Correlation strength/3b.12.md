#### Task Description

A retail manager compares the size of a customer's shoes with the total dollar amount they spent during a store visit. The resulting scatter plot shows data points spread randomly across the graph, with no visible upward or downward slope and no discernible pattern.

What does this pattern indicate about the relationship?

##### Explanation
A "no relationship" or zero correlation scenario is characterized by a random cloud of data points on a scatter plot. If there is no discernible slope (upward or downward) and the points do not follow any linear trend, the variables are not correlated.

##### Domain
Business

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Weak positive relationship — Misinterprets the lack of trend
A weak positive relationship would still show a slight, general upward trend, even if the points are widely dispersed.

###### Option 2
Strong positive relationship — Misinterprets both pattern and strength
A strong positive relationship would show points clustered tightly along an upward-sloping line.

###### Option 3
Strong negative relationship — Misinterprets both direction and strength
A strong negative relationship would show points clustered tightly along a downward-sloping line.
