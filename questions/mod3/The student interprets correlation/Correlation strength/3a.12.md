##### Explanation
A Pearson correlation coefficient of $-0.87$ indicates a strong relationship because its absolute value $(0.87)$ is close to 1. The negative sign indicates the direction: longer load times are associated with lower satisfaction scores. Both strength and direction must be considered when interpreting correlation coefficients.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
A weak correlation would have an absolute value closer to 0, such as $0.15$ or $0.20$. The value $0.87$ is close to 1, indicating strength. This distractor tests whether candidates confuse weak correlations with strong correlations.

###### Option 3
A moderate correlation typically ranges from $0.50$ to $0.60$. A value of $0.87$ is closer to $1$, indicating a strong rather than moderate relationship. This distractor addresses the misconception about thresholds for correlation strength categories.

###### Option 4
This option ignores the negative sign in the correlation coefficient. While the relationship is strong, the direction is negative, meaning the variables move in opposite directions. This distractor tests whether candidates properly interpret both the magnitude and direction of correlation.
