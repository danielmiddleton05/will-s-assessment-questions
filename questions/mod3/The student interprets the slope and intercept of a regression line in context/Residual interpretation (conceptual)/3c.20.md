#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital uses a regression model to predict the length of stay in days after a surgical procedure. For a 50-year-old patient, the model predicted a length of stay of $\hat{y} = 9$ days. After the procedure, this patient had a residual of $-3$ days.

What does the residual indicate about the model's prediction for this patient?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
The patient's actual stay was longer than the predicted 9 days, meaning the model underestimated this patient's recovery time.


###### Option 2 Correct
The patient's actual stay was shorter than the predicted 9 days, meaning the model overestimated this patient's recovery time.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The residual shows the prediction was off by 3 days, but the negative sign alone does not indicate whether the patient stayed longer or shorter than predicted.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The negative residual means the regression model systematically overpredicts by 3 days and should be recalibrated.

##### Explanation
1. **Recall the residual formula:** A residual equals the actual value minus the predicted value: $\text{residual} = y - \hat{y}$.
2. **Interpret the sign of the residual:** A negative residual ($-3$) means the actual value was less than the predicted value, since $y - \hat{y} < 0$ implies $y < \hat{y}$. In this case, $y = 9 + (-3) = 6$ days.
3. **Interpret in context:** The patient's actual stay (6 days) was shorter than the model predicted (9 days). The model overestimated this patient's recovery time — the patient recovered and was discharged faster than the model anticipated.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
This reverses the sign convention of residuals. The student confuses a negative residual with underprediction, believing that the negative sign means the model's prediction fell short. In reality, a negative residual ($y - \hat{y} < 0$) means the actual value was below the prediction, so the model overpredicted, not underpredicted.

###### Option 3
This reflects a misconception that the residual only conveys the magnitude of the error and that the sign is arbitrary or meaningless. In fact, the sign of the residual is informative by definition: a negative residual specifically indicates the actual value was below the predicted value, and a positive residual indicates the actual value was above it. The direction is not ambiguous.

###### Option 4
This overgeneralizes a single patient's residual to the entire model. A residual of $-3$ for one patient describes the prediction error for that individual observation only. It does not imply that the model systematically overpredicts for all patients. Assessing systematic bias would require examining the pattern of residuals across many observations, not a single case.
