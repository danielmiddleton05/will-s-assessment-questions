#### Task Description

**Given:** A regression equation predicts monthly mobile application downloads (in thousands) based on marketing budget (in thousands of dollars):
$\text{Downloads} = 2.4 + 3.1(\text{Budget})$

According to the model, by how much are monthly downloads predicted to change when the marketing budget increases from 2 to 5 thousand dollars?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
3.1 thousand downloads


###### Option 2 Correct
9.3 thousand downloads


###### Option 3 Incorrect
5.5 thousand downloads


###### Option 4 Incorrect
2.4 thousand downloads

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student analyzes relationships between variables in real-world contexts, including correlation and regression.

##### Evidence Statement
The student interprets the slope and intercept of a regression line in context.

##### Aligned Content
Slope as rate of change OR Intercept as baseline

##### Explanation
The slope of the regression equation (3.1) represents the rate of change in the dependent variable (Downloads) for every one-unit increase in the independent variable (Budget). The question asks for the predicted change when the budget increases from \$2,000 to \$5,000, which is an increase of 3 units (since the budget is measured in thousands of dollars). To find the predicted change in downloads, multiply the slope by the change in the independent variable: $3.1 \times 3 = 9.3$. Since the dependent variable is also measured in thousands, this represents a predicted increase of 9.3 thousand downloads.

##### Distractors
##### Distractors
###### Option 1
This value is the slope of the regression line. While it correctly identifies the rate of change for a one-unit increase (\$1,000), it fails to account for the total change of three units (\$3,000) specified in the prompt.

###### Option 3
This value is the sum of the y-intercept (2.4) and the slope (3.1). This is a common calculation error where the student simply adds the two coefficients of the regression equation together without applying the change in the independent variable.

###### Option 4
This value is the y-intercept of the regression equation. The intercept represents the predicted value of the dependent variable when the independent variable is zero (the baseline), rather than the amount of change between two budget levels.
