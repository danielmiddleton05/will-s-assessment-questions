#### Task Description

**Given:** A local bookstore tracks weekly book sales and fits a trend line to the data. The trend line shows that in **Week 5**, 180 books were sold, and sales are increasing at a constant rate of **20 books per week**.

According to this trend line, how many books would be predicted for **Week 8**?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
200 books


###### Option 2 Incorrect
260 books


###### Option 3 Incorrect
300 books


###### Option 4 Correct
240 books

##### Explanation
The trend line predicts 180 books for Week 5. From Week 5 to Week 8 is 3 additional weeks. Using the rate of change (slope) of 20 books per week, the prediction is: $180 + 20(3) = 180 + 60 = 240$ books.

##### Domain
Business

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
200 books — Adds only one week's increase (20) to the Week 5 total instead of three weeks' worth.

###### Option 2
260 books — Miscounts the number of weeks (adds 4 increments of 20 instead of 3).

###### Option 3
300 books — Incorrectly calculates the total increase or adds 60 twice.
