#### Task Description

**Given:** A technology company reports that its servers had a 99.9% uptime rate during the past year.

What does this imply about server downtime during that year?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
The servers experienced no downtime.


###### Option 2 Correct
The servers were unavailable for approximately 0.1% of the measured time.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
There was a 0.1% chance that the servers experienced any downtime.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Exactly 0.1% of the servers failed.

##### Explanation
Uptime refers to the proportion of time a system is functional. An uptime rate of 99.9% means that the system was unavailable (downtime) for the remaining 0.1% of the year. This is a measure of total duration (availability) rather than the probability of a single failure event occurring.

##### Domain

Technology

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
The servers experienced no downtime. — Misinterprets a high uptime rate as a guarantee of absolute perfection (100% uptime).

###### Option 3
There was a 0.1% chance that the servers experienced any downtime. — Confuses the proportion of time (availability) with the probability of a failure event occurring (reliability). In this scenario, the uptime rate is a retrospective report of time elapsed, meaning downtime did occur.

###### Option 4
Exactly 0.1% of the servers failed. — Misinterprets the percentage of time the system was down as a percentage of the total number of physical units that failed.
