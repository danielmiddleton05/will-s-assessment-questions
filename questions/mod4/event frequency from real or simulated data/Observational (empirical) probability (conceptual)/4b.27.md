#### Task Description

**Given:** Over the past five semesters, a college tutoring center recorded that 126 out of 180 students who attended weekly tutoring sessions passed their math course. Based on this data, the center reports that the probability a student attending weekly tutoring will pass is 0.70.

What characteristic of this estimate makes it an example of empirical probability?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Correct
It is calculated from the frequency of outcomes observed in actual student records.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
It assumes each student attending tutoring has an equally likely chance of passing.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
It was derived from a mathematical model of all possible academic outcomes.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
It will produce the same value each time regardless of which student records are examined.

##### Explanation
Empirical probability is defined by its reliance on observed data from real-world events. In this scenario, the tutoring center divided the number of students who passed (126) by the total number of students tracked (180), yielding 0.70. This estimate is empirical because it is derived directly from the frequency of outcomes recorded in actual student data rather than from theoretical assumptions or mathematical models. The defining characteristic is its basis in observed results.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
It assumes each student attending tutoring has an equally likely chance of passing — Describes a characteristic of theoretical probability.
Students may select this if they confuse the basis of empirical probability with the assumptions underlying theoretical probability, where outcomes are treated as equally likely rather than derived from observed frequencies.

###### Option 3
It was derived from a mathematical model of all possible academic outcomes — Describes a theoretical approach to probability.
Students may select this if they believe that any calculation involving division of counts constitutes a mathematical model, failing to distinguish between analyzing observed data and constructing a model of all possible outcomes.

###### Option 4
It will produce the same value each time regardless of which student records are examined — Reflects a misconception about empirical probability.
Students may select this if they believe empirical probability yields a fixed, universal constant. In reality, empirical probability can change depending on the specific data collected, since different samples of students or semesters may produce different observed frequencies.
