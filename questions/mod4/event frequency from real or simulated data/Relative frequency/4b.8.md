#### Task Description

**Given:** A software company conducted two rounds of testing for a new application to measure stability.
- **Round 1:** 500 users participated, and 125 reported experiencing at least one error.
- **Round 2:** 400 users participated, and 60 reported experiencing at least one error.

Which round had a higher relative frequency of reported errors, and what was that frequency?

##### Explanation
To determine which round had the higher relative frequency, calculate the proportion for each:
- **Round 1:** $125 \div 500 = 0.25$
- **Round 2:** $60 \div 400 = 0.15$
Comparing the two, Round 1 has the higher relative frequency ($0.25 > 0.15$).
