#### Task Description

**Given:** A school administrator creates the two-way table shown below, displaying student enrollment by grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th) and participation in athletics (Yes, No). The table shows counts for each combination and totals for each row and column.

In this table, what do the column totals represent?

#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student explains how probability represents uncertainty in real-world situations.

##### Evidence Statement

##### Aligned Content
Two-way tables (joint/marginal/conditional — conceptual)

##### Explanation
In a two-way table, marginal distributions appear in the row totals and column totals, showing the distribution of each variable separately. Column totals show the total students participating versus not participating in athletics—the marginal distribution of athletics participation.

##### Domain

Education

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
The conditional distribution of grade level — Confuses marginal with conditional

Students may select this if they confuse marginal distributions (totals) with conditional distributions (proportions within a specific category of the other variable).

###### Option 2
The sample space of all possible students — Confuses distribution with sample space

Students may select this if they confuse the distribution of observed data with the theoretical concept of all possible outcomes.

###### Option 4
The joint distribution of grade and athletics — Confuses totals with cell values

Students may select this if they confuse the marginal totals with the joint frequencies in the interior cells that show combinations of both variables.
