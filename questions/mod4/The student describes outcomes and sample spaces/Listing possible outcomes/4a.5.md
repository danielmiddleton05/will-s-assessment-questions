**Given:** A hospital's discharge tracking system records a patient's destination after discharge. The system does not distinguish between types of discharge once the patient leaves the hospital. A patient may:

How many distinct destinations are in the sample space for one patient?

##### Explanation
The sample space is the set of all unique possible outcomes for the patient's destination as defined by the tracking system. While the system lists four specific scenarios, they result in only three distinct destinations: Home (which includes both immediate discharge and discharge after rehabilitation), Another hospital, and a Skilled nursing facility. Identifying the sample space requires identifying each unique destination explicitly stated without miscounting the descriptive categories as separate destinations.

##### Domain

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
2 — This targets students who incorrectly reduce the sample space to a binary categorization, such as "home" versus "medical facility." This misconception reflects a tendency to oversimplify outcome spaces rather than recognizing each unique destination category.

###### Option 3
4 — This targets students who count the total number of bulleted scenarios provided in the description (Go home, Go home after rehabilitation, Another hospital, Skilled nursing facility) without recognizing that the first two scenarios result in the same distinct destination: Home.

###### Option 4
5 — This targets students who add an outcome not specified in the scenario, such as "deceased" or "left against medical advice." The sample space must be defined strictly by the outcomes the system is described as recording.
