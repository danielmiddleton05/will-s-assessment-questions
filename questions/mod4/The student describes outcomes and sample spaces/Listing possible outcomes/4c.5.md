#### Task Description

**Given:** A human resources (HR) department uses a tracking system that assigns exactly one status to each job application received.

The possible statuses are:

- Under review
- Interview scheduled
- Offer extended
- Application closed

During the most recent hiring cycle, no applications were assigned the status "Offer extended."

What is the complete sample space for a single application's status?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 2 Incorrect
{Under review, Interview scheduled, Application closed}


###### Option 3 Incorrect
{Under review, Application closed}


###### Option 4 Incorrect
{Under review, Interview scheduled, Offer extended, Application closed, Hired}

##### Explanation
The sample space is the complete set of all possible outcomes as defined by the tracking system, regardless of whether every outcome was observed during a particular period. The system explicitly defines four statuses: Under review, Interview scheduled, Offer extended, and Application closed. Even though no applications were assigned "Offer extended" during the most recent hiring cycle, that status remains a possible outcome within the system and must be included in the sample space. The sample space is determined by what the system is designed to record, not by what happened to occur in one cycle of data.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
{Under review, Interview scheduled, Application closed} — This targets students who remove "Offer extended" because it was not observed during the most recent hiring cycle. This misconception confuses observed data from a single period with the full set of possible outcomes. The sample space is defined by the system's structure, not by which outcomes happened to occur.

###### Option 3
{Under review, Application closed} — This targets students who oversimplify the sample space into a binary categorization, such as "in progress" versus "finished." This reflects a tendency to collapse multiple distinct statuses into broad groupings rather than listing each outcome defined by the system.

###### Option 4
{Under review, Interview scheduled, Offer extended, Application closed, Hired} — This targets students who add an outcome not defined by the tracking system. While "Hired" may be a logical next step in a hiring process, it is not one of the statuses the system is described as assigning. The sample space must be defined strictly by the outcomes the system records.
