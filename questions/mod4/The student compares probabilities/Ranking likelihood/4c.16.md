**Given:** A school district evaluates the on-time graduation likelihood for students in four academic support programs:

##### Explanation
To compare the graduation probabilities, each value must be expressed in a common format. Mentor Program: 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 = 75%. Peer Tutoring: 0.80 = 80%. Study Groups: 57 ÷ 76 = 0.75 = 75% (since 57/76 simplifies to 3/4). Online Modules: 72%. With all values converted, Mentor Program and Study Groups both equal 75%, and both exceed Online Modules at 72%. Therefore, the correct statement is that Mentor Program and Study Groups share the same graduation probability and both are higher than Online Modules.
