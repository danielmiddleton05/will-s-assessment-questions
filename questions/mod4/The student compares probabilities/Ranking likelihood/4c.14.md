##### Explanation
To compare these probabilities, each must be converted to a common format. Converting all to decimals: career prospects = 3/10 = 0.30, academic difficulty = 15% = 0.15, loss of interest = 0.35, and schedule conflicts = 1/5 = 0.20. Because 0.35 is the largest value, loss of interest is the most likely reason a student changes their major. Because 0.15 is the smallest value, academic difficulty is the least likely reason.

##### Distractors

###### Option 3
Most likely: Career prospects; Least likely: Academic difficulty — This targets students who correctly identify academic difficulty (0.15) as the least likely reason but incorrectly select career prospects (0.30) as the most likely, confusing it with loss of interest (0.35) or seeing "3 out of 10" as larger without carefully comparing the converted values.

###### Option 4
Most likely: Loss of interest; Least likely: Schedule conflicts — This targets students who correctly identify loss of interest (0.35) as the most likely reason but incorrectly select schedule conflicts (1/5 = 0.20) as the least likely, perhaps misreading 15% as 0.15 in percentage form only and not recognizing that 0.15 is less than 0.20.
