##### Explanation
To find the relative frequency, first calculate the total number of observations: $162 + 28 + 10 = 200$. Next, identify the frequency of the event in question ("late" deliveries includes both "1 day late" and "2+ days late"): $28 + 10 = 38$. Finally, divide the event frequency by the total: $38 \div 200 = 0.19$. Option 2 calculates the frequency of on-time deliveries ($162 \div 200$). Option 3 only calculates the relative frequency for "1 day late" ($28 \div 200$). Option 4 only calculates the relative frequency for "2+ days late" ($10 \div 200$).
