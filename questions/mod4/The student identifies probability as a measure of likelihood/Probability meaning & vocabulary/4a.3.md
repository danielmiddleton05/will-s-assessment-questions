#### Task Description

**Given:** A hospital reports that the probability of a patient experiencing side effects from a new medication is 0.15. A second hospital reports that 85% of their patients did NOT experience side effects from the same medication.

What can be concluded about the likelihood of side effects at these two hospitals?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Correct

###### Option 2 Incorrect
The first hospital has a higher likelihood of side effects.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
The second hospital has a higher likelihood of side effects.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The probabilities cannot be compared because they are expressed differently.

##### Explanation
This problem requires two steps: (1) recognizing that 0.15 represents a 15% probability of side effects, and (2) understanding that if 85% did NOT experience side effects, then 100% − 85% = 15% DID experience them. Both hospitals report the same 15% likelihood of side effects—probability can be expressed in equivalent ways.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
The first hospital has a higher likelihood of side effects — Fails to convert the complement
This targets students who correctly convert 0.15 to 15% but fail to recognize that 85% refers to patients WITHOUT side effects. They may incorrectly compare 15% to 85% without taking the complement.

###### Option 3
The second hospital has a higher likelihood of side effects — Confuses the complement for the event
This targets students who see the larger number (85%) and assume it represents the likelihood of side effects, without recognizing that it describes patients who did NOT experience them.

###### Option 4
The probabilities cannot be compared because they are expressed differently — Believes different formats are incomparable
This targets students who don't recognize that probabilities can be converted between decimals and percentages, and that complementary events can be used to find equivalent likelihoods.
