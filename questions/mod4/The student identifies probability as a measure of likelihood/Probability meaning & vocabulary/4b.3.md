#### Task Description

**Given:** The probability of selecting a blue marble from a jar is 0.25.

What does this probability indicate about selecting a blue marble?

Blue marbles make up 0.25% of the marbles in the jar.

##### Explanation
A probability of 0.25 means there is a 25% chance, or equivalently a 1 in 4 chance ($1 \div 4 = 0.25$), of the event occurring. This represents the likelihood or expected long-run frequency of selecting a blue marble. It does not specify the exact count of marbles, and it requires proper conversion to fractions or percentages.

##### Domain
Education

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
The jar contains exactly 25 blue marbles — Confuses probability with count. Students may select this if they interpret the decimal 0.25 as representing a specific quantity of objects rather than a proportion or likelihood.

###### Option 3
There is a 1 in 25 chance of selecting a blue marble — Confuses the decimal value with the denominator of a fraction. Students may mistakenly use the digits of the decimal (25) as the total number of outcomes.

###### Option 4
Blue marbles make up 0.25% of the marbles in the jar — Fails to correctly convert a decimal probability to a percentage. Students may select this if they forget that 0.25 is equivalent to 25%, not 0.25%.
