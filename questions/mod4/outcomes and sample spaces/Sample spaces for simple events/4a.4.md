##### Explanation
A sample space consists of all possible outcomes of an event. For an application decision with three defined categories, the sample space must include exactly those three: Accept, Waitlist, and Deny. It must be exhaustive (containing all possible results) and exclusive (containing only the results possible within the defined scenario).

##### Domain

Education

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
{Accept, Deny} — Incomplete sample space
This targets students who omit one of the defined possible outcomes (Waitlist). An incomplete sample space fails to account for all possibilities mentioned in the scenario.

###### Option 2
{Accept, Waitlist, Pending} — Incorrect categories
This targets students who fail to match the outcomes to the specific categories provided. It omits "Deny" and includes "Pending," which was not defined as a possible decision in the stem. This ensures students cannot select the answer simply by counting the number of elements.

###### Option 3
{Accept, Waitlist, Deny, None} — Over-inclusive sample space
This targets students who believe a sample space should include a "null" or "none" category. It tests the understanding that a sample space should only contain the specific outcomes defined by the event's parameters.
