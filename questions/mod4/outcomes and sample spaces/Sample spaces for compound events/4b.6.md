#### Task Description

**Given:** A lab technician records both the ABO blood type (A, B, AB, or O) and the Rh factor (positive or negative) for a donor. These two traits are independent of each other.

What is the sample space for this situation?

##### Explanation
The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes. When two characteristics are recorded simultaneously (ABO type and Rh factor), an outcome must represent a combination of both. Since there are 4 blood types and 2 Rh factors, and the traits are independent, the fundamental counting principle indicates there are 4 × 2 = 8 possible outcomes: {A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-}.

##### Domain
Health

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
{A, B, AB, O} — Incomplete sample space. This only lists the ABO types and fails to account for the Rh factor.

###### Option 2
{+, -} — Incomplete sample space. This only lists the Rh factors and fails to account for the ABO blood type.

###### Option 3
{A, B, AB, O, +, -} — Confuses the union of individual attribute sets with the set of combined outcomes. A single patient outcome must be a combination (e.g., A+), not a single attribute from one of the two categories.
