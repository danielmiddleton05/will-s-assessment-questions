#### Task Description

**Given:** A technology company created the following frequency table showing employee work arrangements:

| Work Location | Number of Employees |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Office        | 120                 |
| Remote        | 200                 |
| Hybrid        | 80                  |

Based on the frequency table, which statement accurately describes the relationship between the likelihoods of the work arrangements?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Correct
Remote work is as likely as all other arrangements combined.


###### Option 2 Incorrect
Office work is more likely than remote work.


###### Option 3 Incorrect
Hybrid work is the most likely arrangement.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
Non-remote work is more likely than remote work.

##### Explanation
To evaluate the relationships between arrangements, first determine the total number of employees and the likelihood of each category:
Total = $120 + 200 + 80 = 400$.
1. **Option 1 (Correct):** The number of remote employees is 200. The combined number of employees in other arrangements (Office + Hybrid) is $120 + 80 = 200$. Since $200 = 200$, selecting a remote employee is as likely ($0.50$ probability) as selecting one from any other arrangement ($0.50$ probability).
2. **Option 2:** Office work (120) has a lower frequency than remote work (200), making it less likely ($0.30$ vs $0.50$).
3. **Option 3:** Hybrid work (80) has the lowest frequency, making it the least likely arrangement ($0.20$).
4. **Option 4:** "Non-remote" refers to the sum of Office and Hybrid ($120 + 80 = 200$). Since the frequency of remote work is also 200, non-remote work is equally likely to, not more likely than, remote work.
