#### Internal Notes

##### Competency
The student applies quantitative operations to solve real-world problems.

##### Evidence Statement
The student simplifies expressions with exponents and square roots.

##### Aligned Content
Evaluating exponential and root expressions

##### Explanation
Evaluate each component separately: $3^2 = 9$ and $\sqrt{64} = 8$. Then multiply the results: $9 \times 8 = 72$.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Targets students who add instead of multiply. Computing $3^2 + \sqrt{64} = 9 + 8 = 17$ confuses the multiplication operation with addition.

###### Option 2
Targets students who calculate $3 \times \sqrt{64}$ without squaring the 3. Computing $3 \times 8 = 24$ ignores the exponent operation.

###### Option 4
Targets students who evaluate the exponent but forget to take the square root. Computing $3^2 \times 64 = 9 \times 64 = 576$ neglects the radical symbol.
