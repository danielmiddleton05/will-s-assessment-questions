##### Explanation
This question assesses the ability to correctly evaluate a square root expression, which is commonly used in business calculations such as standard deviation, variance, and efficiency metrics. To solve: $\sqrt{196}$ asks "what number multiplied by itself equals 196?" Since $14 \times 14 = 196$, the answer is 14.
