##### Explanation
The problem requires evaluating the expression $2^3 \times 5^2$. According to the order of operations, both exponents must be simplified before multiplying.

1. Evaluate the first exponent: $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$ (the number of storage clusters).
2. Evaluate the second exponent: $5^2 = 5 \times 5 = 25$ (terabytes per cluster).
3. Multiply to find the total: $8 \times 25 = 200$ terabytes.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
33 — Adds the results instead of multiplying. The student correctly evaluates $2^3 = 8$ and $5^2 = 25$ but adds them ($8 + 25 = 33$) instead of multiplying to find the total capacity.

###### Option 2
60 — Treats exponents as multiplication of the base and the exponent. The student computes $2^3$ as $2 \times 3 = 6$ and $5^2$ as $5 \times 2 = 10$, then multiplies $6 \times 10 = 60$, misinterpreting exponent notation for both terms.

###### Option 3
150 — Partially misinterprets exponent notation. The student computes $2^3$ as $2 \times 3 = 6$ (misreading the exponent as multiplication) but correctly evaluates $5^2 = 25$, then multiplies $6 \times 25 = 150$.
