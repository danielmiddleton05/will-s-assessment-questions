#### Task Description

A laboratory technician is monitoring a bacterial culture for a medical study. At 10:00 AM, the culture contains 40 bacteria. The population is observed to double every hour. 

How many bacteria will be in the culture at 3:00 PM?

##### Explanation
This question assesses the ability to model a real-world exponential growth scenario and evaluate the resulting expression. The student must recognize that from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM there are 5 growth intervals, meaning the bacteria count at 3:00 PM is $40 \times 2^5$. Evaluate the exponent: $2^5 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 32$. Then multiply: $40 \times 32 = 1,280$. The correct answer is 1,280.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
400 — Targets students who multiply the base by the exponent ($2 \times 5 = 10$) instead of evaluating $2^5 = 32$, then compute $40 \times 10 = 400$.

###### Option 2
640 — Targets students who miscount the number of growth intervals, using an exponent of 4 instead of 5 (effectively calculating the 2:00 PM count), computing $40 \times 2^4 = 40 \times 16 = 640$.

###### Option 4
2,560 — Targets students who use 6 as the exponent because 3:00 PM is the 6th timestamp in the sequence (10, 11, 12, 1, 2, 3), computing $40 \times 2^6 = 40 \times 64 = 2,560$, instead of recognizing that only 5 growth intervals occur.
