##### Explanation
To determine the listing agent's commission, first find the total number of parts in the ratio: 3 + 2 = 5 parts. Then divide the total commission by the number of parts to find the value of one part: $24,000 ÷ 5 = $4,800 per part. Finally, multiply by the listing agent's ratio portion: $4,800 × 3 = $14,400. This demonstrates understanding of how to partition a total amount according to a given ratio, a fundamental skill in business contexts involving proportional distributions.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
$8,000 — Targets students who divide the total by the listing agent's ratio number instead of the sum of ratio parts (24,000 ÷ 3 = 8,000), demonstrating confusion about how to work with ratio notation when finding individual portions.

###### Option 2
$9,600 — Targets students who correctly apply the proportional reasoning process but calculate the selling agent's share instead of the listing agent's share (2/5 × 24,000 = 9,600), demonstrating confusion about which portion of the ratio corresponds to which party.

###### Option 3
$12,000 — Targets students who multiply the ratio numbers instead of adding them when finding total parts (3 × 2 = 6), then divide and multiply incorrectly: 24,000 ÷ 6 = 4,000, then 3 × 4,000 = 12,000. This reflects a fundamental misunderstanding of how ratio parts combine to represent a whole.
