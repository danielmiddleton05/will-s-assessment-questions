##### Explanation
To solve this problem, first find the download rate by dividing megabytes by seconds: 18 MB ÷ 12 seconds = 1.5 MB per second. Then multiply this rate by the total time: 1.5 MB/second × 28 seconds = 42 MB. The proportional relationship can be expressed as $d = 1.5t$, where $d$ is megabytes downloaded and $t$ is time in seconds.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
34 MB — Uses additive reasoning instead of proportional reasoning
The student calculates the additional time (28 - 12 = 16 seconds) and incorrectly assumes each additional second adds 1 MB instead of 1.5 MB. Adding 16 to the initial 18 MB gives 34 MB. This error reflects a misunderstanding of the constant rate as a one-to-one relationship rather than computing the actual unit rate.

###### Option 2
36 MB — Applies approximate doubling without precise calculation
The student estimates that 28 seconds is roughly double 12 seconds (actually 28 ÷ 12 ≈ 2.33) and simply doubles the 18 MB to get 36 MB. This error shows recognition that the relationship involves multiplication but fails to calculate the exact scale factor or unit rate.

###### Option 4
46 MB — Adds the given numerical values
The student adds the two numbers provided in the problem (18 + 28 = 46) without regard to their units or the proportional relationship. This error reflects a failure to recognize that megabytes and seconds cannot be combined through addition and indicates confusion about how to set up the proportion.
