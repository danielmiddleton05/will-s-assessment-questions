##### Explanation
To round 84.652 to the nearest tenth, examine the digit in the hundredths place (5). According to rounding rules, when the digit is 5 or greater, round up. Therefore, the 6 in the tenths place rounds up to 7, making the answer 84.7.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
84.5 — Targets students who confuse place values and mistakenly place the hundredths digit (5) into the tenths position of their answer, rather than using it as the decision digit to determine whether to round up or down.

###### Option 2
84.6 — Targets students who truncate rather than round, simply dropping all digits after the tenths place without applying proper rounding rules, or who incorrectly believe that when the deciding digit is exactly 5, they should round down.

###### Option 4
84.8 — Targets students who incorrectly apply cascade rounding, first rounding the tenths digit up (6→7) based on the hundredths digit, then erroneously rounding again (7→8) as if each rounding step triggers another adjustment.
