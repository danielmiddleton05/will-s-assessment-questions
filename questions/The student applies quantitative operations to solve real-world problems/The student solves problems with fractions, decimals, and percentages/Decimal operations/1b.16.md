Decimal operations

##### Explanation
This question assesses the ability to work backward with decimal operations to find an initial value given a final value and intermediate transactions. Unlike a direct addition problem, this requires students to recognize that to find the starting amount, they must add the purchases back to the remaining amount: $126.25 + $34.75 + $18.50 = $179.50. Students must align decimal points correctly and handle carrying when adding the cents column (25 + 75 + 50 = 150 cents = $1.50) and the dollars column (126 + 34 + 18 = 178, plus the carried dollar = 179).

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
\$73.00 — Targets students who apply the wrong operation. Instead of adding the purchases back to find the initial amount, they subtract: $126.25 - $34.75 - $18.50 = $73.00. This reflects a fundamental misunderstanding of working backwards from a final value.

###### Option 2
\$179.00 — Targets students who make an error in the cents column when adding. When computing 25 + 75 + 50 = 150 cents, a place value or carrying error results in recording $1.00 instead of $1.50, leading to a total of $179.00 instead of $179.50.

###### Option 4
\$180.50 — Targets students who incorrectly carry an extra dollar when adding. An arithmetic error in managing the carry from the cents to the dollars column (or in the dollars column itself) inflates the total by $1.00, resulting in $180.50.
