$$

What is the threat score for this vulnerability?

##### Explanation
The threat score is $(9 \times 0) \div 3$. First, $9 \times 0 = 0$ because any number multiplied by zero equals zero. Then, $0 \div 3 = 0$ because zero divided by any non-zero number equals zero. Unlike the original question, which tests a single division operation with zero as the numerator, this variation requires multi-step reasoning: the student must first evaluate a product involving zero and then carry that result through a division. This assesses understanding of how zero behaves across both multiplication and division in a practical cybersecurity vulnerability scoring context.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
3 — The student computes $9 \div 3 = 3$, effectively skipping the multiplication step or treating the impact rating of 0 as though it has no effect on the calculation. This error reflects a failure to recognize that multiplying by zero makes the entire product zero, regardless of the other factor's value.

###### Option 3
9 — The student selects only the exploitability rating, treating the impact rating of 0 as though it were 1 (the multiplicative identity) and also ignoring the division by 3. This reflects a misunderstanding of the zero property of multiplication, where the student may believe that multiplying by zero "does nothing" to the original number.

###### Option 4
Undefined — The student sees zero in the formula and incorrectly concludes the entire expression is undefined. This confuses zero appearing as a factor in the numerator (which produces a valid result of zero) with zero appearing as a divisor (which would be undefined). Students may recall that "zero causes problems in calculations" without understanding that the issue only arises when zero is the divisor.
