**Given:** A statistician analyzed patient wait times across four hospital departments and calculated the following mean wait times: Emergency = 2.5 hours, Radiology = 2.35 hours, Laboratory = 2.45 hours, Pharmacy = 2.4 hours.

What is the correct order of departments from shortest to longest mean wait time?

To order the mean wait times correctly, convert all decimals to the same precision for comparison: Radiology = 2.35 hours, Pharmacy = 2.40 hours, Laboratory = 2.45 hours, Emergency = 2.50 hours. Arranging from shortest to longest gives: Radiology, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Emergency.
