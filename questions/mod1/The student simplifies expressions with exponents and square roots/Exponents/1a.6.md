##### Explanation
The problem describes the expression $50 \times 3^4$. According to the order of operations, the exponential term (the multiplier) must be evaluated first.

1.  Calculate the multiplier: $3^4 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 81$.
2.  Multiply by the initial deposit: $50 \times 81 = 4{,}050$.

The future value of the investment is **$4,050**.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
600 — Treats "raised to the power of" as multiplication. The student calculates $50 \times (3 \times 4) = 600$, incorrectly simplifying the exponent.

###### Option 2
650 — Adds quantities instead of using exponential multiplication. The student may calculate $50 + (3 \times 4 \times 50) = 650$.

###### Option 3
1,350 — Represents an incomplete exponentiation error. The student calculates $3^3 = 27$ instead of $3^4 = 81$, resulting in $50 \times 27 = 1,350$.
