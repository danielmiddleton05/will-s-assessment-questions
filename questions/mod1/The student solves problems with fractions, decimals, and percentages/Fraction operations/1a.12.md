##### Explanation
To add $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{4}$, find a common denominator of $20$: $\frac{2}{5} = \frac{8}{20}$ and $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{20}$. Adding: $\frac{8}{20} + \frac{5}{20} = \frac{13}{20}$.

##### Distractors

###### Option 2

###### Option 3
