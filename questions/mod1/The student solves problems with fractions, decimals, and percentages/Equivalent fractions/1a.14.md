##### Explanation
To simplify $\frac{45}{75}$, find the greatest common factor (GCF) of $45$ and $75$, which is $15$. Dividing both numerator and denominator by $15$ yields: $45 \div 15 = 3$ and $75 \div 15 = 5$, giving the fraction $\frac{3}{5}$ in lowest terms.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
$\frac{9}{25}$ — Inconsistent simplification
Student divides the numerator by $5$ (getting $45 \div 5 = 9$) and the denominator by $3$ (getting $75 \div 3 = 25$), applying different factors to each rather than dividing both by the same common factor.

###### Option 2
$\frac{1}{2}$ — Estimates incorrectly
Student approximates the fraction without calculating, thinking $45$ out of $75$ is "about half." This is incorrect because $\frac{3}{5} = 0.6$ or $60\%$, while $\frac{1}{2} = 0.5$ or $50\%$.

###### Option 4
$\frac{5}{3}$ — Inverted fraction
Student correctly identifies the simplified values of $3$ and $5$ but swaps the numerator and denominator, placing $5$ over $3$, confusing which original number corresponds to which part of the fraction.
