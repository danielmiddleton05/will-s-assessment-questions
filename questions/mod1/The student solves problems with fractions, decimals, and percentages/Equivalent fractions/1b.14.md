#### Task Description

Two branch managers are comparing their customer retention rates. Branch A retained $35$ out of $56$ new customers. Branch B retained $5$ out of $8$ new customers.

Which statement accurately compares the retention rates of the two branches?

###### Option 2 Incorrect
Branch B has a higher retention rate.


###### Option 3 Correct
Both branches have equivalent retention rates.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The rates cannot be compared because the total number of customers is different.

##### Explanation
To compare the retention rates, determine if the fractions $\frac{35}{56}$ and $\frac{5}{8}$ are equivalent.
Simplify the fraction for Branch A by dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor (GCF), which is $7$:
$\frac{35 \div 7}{56 \div 7} = \frac{5}{8}$.
Since $\frac{35}{56}$ simplifies to $\frac{5}{8}$, the retention rates for both branches are equivalent.

##### Distractors

**Branch A has a higher retention rate.**
This error may occur if the student compares the numerators ($35 > 5$) or denominators ($56 > 8$) directly without considering the ratio, or if they estimate incorrectly that $\frac{35}{56}$ is greater than $\frac{5}{8}$.

###### Option 2
**Branch B has a higher retention rate.**
This error may result from an arithmetic mistake when simplifying $\frac{35}{56}$ (e.g., thinking it simplifies to something less than $\frac{5}{8}$) or assuming that the simplified "cleaner" fraction represents a better performance automatically.

###### Option 4
**The rates cannot be compared because the total number of customers is different.**
This results from a conceptual misunderstanding of ratios and proportions. The student believes that fractions can only be compared if the denominators (total populations) are identical, failing to recognize that rates allow for comparison across different sample sizes.
