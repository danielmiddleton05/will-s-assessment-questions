##### Explanation
To convert $\frac{3}{5}$ to a percentage: $3 \div 5 = 0.6$, and $0.6 \times 100 = 60\%$.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
15% — Targets students who multiply instead of divide. Students compute $3 \times 5 = 15$ and write 15% instead of correctly dividing $3 \div 5 = 0.6$ to get 60%.

###### Option 2
35% — Targets students who concatenate the numerator and denominator. Students write "3" and "5" together as 35, misunderstanding the conversion process and avoiding division altogether.

###### Option 3
53% — Targets students who reverse or combine digits incorrectly. Students may write the fraction as a two-digit number by combining 5 and 3 as "53" without performing the required division operation.
