##### Explanation
The problem translates to the expression (\$800 + \$200 × 5) ÷ 2. Following order of operations (PEMDAS), first perform the multiplication: \$200 × 5 = \$1,000. Then perform the addition inside the parentheses: \$800 + \$1,000 = \$1,800. Finally, divide by 2: \$1,800 ÷ 2 = \$900. The student receives \$900 per installment.
