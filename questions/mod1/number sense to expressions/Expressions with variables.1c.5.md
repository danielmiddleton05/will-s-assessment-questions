**Given:** A physical therapy clinic charges a \$180 initial evaluation fee plus \$50 per therapy session.

##### Explanation
Model the total cost as $50s + 180$, where $s$ is the number of sessions. Setting this equal to the total bill: $50s + 180 = 680$. Solving for $s$: $50s = 680 - 180 = 500$, therefore $s = 500 ÷ 50 = 10$ sessions.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
8 sessions — Makes arithmetic error in subtraction
Student incorrectly calculates 680 - 180 = 400 (instead of 500), then divides 400 ÷ 50 = 8 sessions.

###### Option 3
14 sessions — Forgets to subtract the evaluation fee
Student divides the total bill directly by the per-session rate: 680 ÷ 50 = 13.6, then rounds up to 14 sessions, omitting the $180 fixed fee from the calculation.

###### Option 4
17 sessions — Adds instead of subtracts the evaluation fee
Student incorrectly calculates (680 + 180) ÷ 50 = 860 ÷ 50 = 17.2, then rounds to 17 sessions, reversing the operation needed to isolate the variable term.
