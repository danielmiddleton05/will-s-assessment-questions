##### Explanation
To find the best estimate of the average, approximate each wait time to a nearby round number and then calculate the average of these estimates. The actual total is 120 minutes (17 + 33 + 26 + 44), and the actual average is 30 minutes (120 ÷ 4).

Looking at the wait times: 17 minutes is close to 20, 33 minutes is close to 30 or 35, 26 minutes is close to 25, and 44 minutes is close to 45. A reasonable approximation gives 20 + 30 + 25 + 45 = 120 minutes total, which divided by 4 patients equals 30 minutes per patient. This matches the actual average.

###### Option 3
35 minutes — This estimate is too high. A student selecting this option may have rounded most values upward (such as treating 17 as 20, 26 as 30, 33 as 40, and 44 as 50), producing a total around 140 minutes and an average that exceeds the actual wait time.

###### Option 4
