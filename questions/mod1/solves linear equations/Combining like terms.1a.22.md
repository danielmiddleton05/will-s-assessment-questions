##### Explanation
Distributing the $-2$: $5x + 14 - 2x - 2 = 27$. Combining like terms: $5x - 2x + 14 - 2 = 27 \rightarrow 3x + 12 = 27$. Subtracting $12$ from both sides: $3x = 15$. Dividing both sides by $3$: $x = 5$.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Targets students who correctly simplify to $3x = 15$ but then divide by the wrong coefficient (dividing $15$ by $5$ instead of by $3$), resulting in $3$.

###### Option 2
Targets students who distribute the $-2$ to the $x$ term but fail to distribute it to the $+1$ inside the parentheses, writing $-2(x + 1) = -2x + 1$ instead of $-2x - 2$, which leads to $3x + 15 = 27$, then $3x = 12$, and finally $x = 4$.

###### Option 4
Targets students who correctly combine like terms to get $3x + 12 = 27$ but then add $12$ to the right side instead of subtracting it (calculating $27 + 12 = 39$), then dividing by $3$ to get $13$.
