#### Task Description

**Given:** A research study pays each participant $20 for completing interviews. The study also has a one-time $40 administrative fee. The total study cost for n participants is $200. This is represented by the equation: 
$$
20n + 40 = 200
$$

What is the value of $n$?

##### Explanation
To solve for $n$, first isolate the variable term by subtracting 40 from both sides: $20n + 40 = 200$ becomes $20n = 160$. Then divide both sides by 20 to find $n = 8$. This indicates there are 8 participants in the study.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
$4$ — Confuses the coefficient and constant. Targets students who swap the values when solving, dividing by 40 instead of 20, computing $(200-40) \div 40 = 4$.

###### Option 3
$10$ — Ignores the constant term. Targets students who divide the total directly by the coefficient, computing $200 \div 20 = 10$ without first subtracting the administrative fee.

###### Option 4
$12$ — Adds instead of subtracts. Targets students who incorrectly add 40 to both sides, solving $20n = 240$, then $n = 12$.
