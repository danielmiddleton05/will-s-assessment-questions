#### Task Description

**Given:** A technology startup budgets for cloud infrastructure. The monthly cost (in thousands of dollars) for maintaining $x$ servers is broken down as follows:

- Compute fees: $6x$
- Platform licensing: $15$
- Volume discount: $-4x$
- Technical support: $25$

If the total monthly cost is $100$ thousand dollars, what is the value of $x$?

##### Explanation
Setting up the equation from the listed cost components: $6x + 15 + (-4x) + 25 = 100$. Combining the variable terms: $6x - 4x = 2x$. Combining the constant terms: $15 + 25 = 40$. This gives the simplified equation $2x + 40 = 100$. Subtracting $40$ from both sides: $2x = 60$. Dividing both sides by $2$: $x = 30$.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Targets students who correctly simplify to $2x + 40 = 100$ and arrive at $2x = 60$, but then divide by $6$ (the leading coefficient from the compute fees line) instead of the combined coefficient $2$, calculating $60 \div 6 = 10$.

###### Option 3
Targets students who correctly combine the variable terms to get $2x$ but neglect to account for the constant terms ($15$ and $25$), effectively solving $2x = 100$ and arriving at $x = 50$.

###### Option 4
Targets students who correctly simplify to $2x + 40 = 100$ but add $40$ to the right side instead of subtracting it, computing $2x = 140$ and dividing by $2$ to get $x = 70$.
