##### Explanation
The inequality $t \le -5$ includes $-5$ (indicated by "or equal to"), so a closed circle is used. Since $t$ must be less than or equal to $-5$, the shading extends to the left, representing all values smaller than $-5$. This type of graphical representation is essential for interpreting acceptable ranges in health-related monitoring and safety constraints.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Targets students who correctly identify the direction of shading but use an open circle, which represents strict inequality ($<$) rather than $\le$. This reflects confusion about whether to include the boundary value.

###### Option 2
Targets students who correctly use a closed circle but shade in the wrong direction, representing $t \ge -5$ instead of $t \le -5$. This indicates confusion about the direction of the inequality symbol.

###### Option 4
Targets students who make both errors: using an open circle and shading in the wrong direction, representing $t > -5$ instead of $t \le -5$. This reflects fundamental misunderstanding of both the boundary inclusion and inequality direction.
