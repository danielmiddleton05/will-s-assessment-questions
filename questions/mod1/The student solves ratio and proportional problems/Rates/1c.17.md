##### Explanation
The program assigns books at a rate of 3 books every 2 weeks. To find the total over 18 weeks, first determine how many 2-week periods fit into 18 weeks: 18 ÷ 2 = 9 periods. Then multiply by the number of books per period: 9 × 3 = 27 books. Alternatively, set up the proportion 3/2 = x/18 and solve: x = 3 × 18 ÷ 2 = 27 books.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Represents the error of dividing 18 by 2 to get 9 but stopping there, finding only the number of 2-week periods without multiplying by the 3 books assigned each period. This reflects incomplete proportional reasoning where the student performs only part of the required calculation.

###### Option 2
Represents the error of inverting the ratio, calculating 2/3 × 18 = 12 instead of 3/2 × 18 = 27. This reflects a common mistake of confusing which value belongs in the numerator versus the denominator when setting up the rate, swapping the books-to-weeks relationship.

###### Option 4
Represents the error of miscounting the number of 2-week periods by including an extra period at the boundary (for example, counting both the starting and ending points), calculating 10 periods instead of 9, then multiplying 10 × 3 = 30. This reflects an off-by-one error when determining how many complete intervals fit within the total timeframe.
