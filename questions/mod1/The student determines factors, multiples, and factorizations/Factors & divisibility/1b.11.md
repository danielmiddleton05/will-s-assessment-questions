##### Explanation
To distribute licenses evenly with none remaining, the number of client sites must be a factor of 180. Testing 17: $180 \div 17 = 10.588...$, which is not a whole number, meaning 17 sites would receive 10 licenses each (170 total), leaving 10 licenses remaining. In contrast: $180 \div 9 = 20$, $180 \div 12 = 15$, and $180 \div 15 = 12$, all yielding whole numbers with no remainder.
