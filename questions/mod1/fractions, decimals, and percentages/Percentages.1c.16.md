#### Task Description

**Given:** A software company's monthly recurring revenue increased from \$80,000 to \$92,000, while a competitor's monthly recurring revenue increased from \$150,000 to \$169,500.

How do the percent increases compare between the two companies?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 2 Incorrect

###### Option 3 Incorrect
Both companies had equal percent increases of 14%.


###### Option 4 Incorrect
The software company had a 13% increase; the competitor had a 12% increase.

##### Explanation
For the software company: Percent increase = (92,000 − 80,000) ÷ 80,000 × 100 = 12,000 ÷ 80,000 × 100 = 15%. For the competitor: Percent increase = (169,500 − 150,000) ÷ 150,000 × 100 = 19,500 ÷ 150,000 × 100 = 13%. Therefore, the software company had a 15% increase and the competitor had a 13% increase.

##### Distractors

###### Option 2
The software company had a 13% increase; the competitor had a 15% increase — Student reverses the calculations or assumes that the larger absolute dollar increase (\$19,500 for competitor vs. \$12,000 for software company) corresponds to a larger percent increase, confusing absolute growth with relative growth.

###### Option 3
Both companies had equal percent increases of 14% — Student incorrectly averages the two percent increases (15% + 13%) ÷ 2 = 14%, or assumes similar growth patterns mean equal percentages without performing the calculations for each company separately.

###### Option 4
The software company had a 13% increase; the competitor had a 12% increase — Student incorrectly uses the new revenue value as the denominator instead of the original value. For the software company: 12,000 ÷ 92,000 × 100 ≈ 13%. For the competitor: 19,500 ÷ 169,500 × 100 ≈ 12%.
