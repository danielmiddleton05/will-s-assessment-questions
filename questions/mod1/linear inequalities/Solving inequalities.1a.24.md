#### Task Description

**Given:** A budget constraint requires that monthly expenses must be less than or equal to $3,500. Current fixed expenses are $1,250, and variable expenses are $75 per unit produced.

What is the maximum number of whole units that can be produced?

##### Explanation
To solve this inequality, first isolate the variable term: $1,250 + 75u \le 3,500$. Subtract $1,250$ from both sides: $75u \le 2,250$. Divide both sides by $75$: $u \le 30$. Since $u$ must be a whole number and the inequality requires $u$ to be less than or equal to $30$, the maximum number of whole units is $30$.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
Results from correctly setting up and solving the inequality ($u \le 30$) but misinterpreting "less than or equal to" as "strictly less than." This targets students who believe $u = 30$ would violate the constraint because expenses would equal (not be less than) $3,500$, leading them to choose $29$ as the maximum.

###### Option 3
Results from dividing $3,500 \div 75 \approx 46.67$, then rounding down to $46$. This targets students who skip the critical step of subtracting the $1,250 fixed expenses before solving for the number of units.

###### Option 4
Results from dividing the total budget by the variable cost ($3,500 \div 75 \approx 46.67$) and rounding up to $47$. This targets students who skip the step of subtracting fixed expenses and also incorrectly round up, exceeding the budget constraint.
