##### Explanation
To determine when both events will next occur on the same day, we need to find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of $18$ and $24$. Using prime factorization: $18 = 2 \times 3^2$ and $24 = 2^3 \times 3$. The LCM takes the highest power of each prime factor: $\text{LCM} = 2^3 \times 3^2 = 8 \times 9 = 72$. Therefore, both events will next occur together again in $72$ days.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
This answer results from doubling the follow-up appointment period ($18 \times 2 = 36$), incorrectly assuming that two cycles of the shorter period will align with the lab panel schedule without verifying that $36$ is a multiple of $24$.

###### Option 2
This is the sum of the two periods ($18 + 24 = 42$). The student incorrectly assumes that adding the cycle lengths will determine when events coincide, showing a fundamental misconception about periodic events.

###### Option 4
This is the product of the two periods ($18 \times 24 = 432$). The student applies the correct method for finding LCM when numbers are coprime (have no common factors) but fails to recognize that $18$ and $24$ share common factors, leading to an unnecessarily large answer.
