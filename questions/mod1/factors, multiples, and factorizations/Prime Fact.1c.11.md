##### Explanation
To find the total number of stores, evaluate the expression: $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 4 \times 9 \times 5 = 36 \times 5 = 180$. This question requires understanding that prime factorization can be evaluated by calculating the powers first, then multiplying the results. The expression uses prime factors ($2$, $3$, and $5$) raised to various powers, which when multiplied together yield the total.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Targets students who incorrectly evaluate exponents by using the base instead of calculating the power. Computing $2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 2 \times 9 \times 5 = 90$ incorrectly treats $2^2$ as just $2$, demonstrating a misunderstanding of exponent notation in prime factorization.

###### Option 2

This requires both misinterpreting $2^2$ as $2^3$ and treating $3^2$ as simply $3$, demonstrating confusion with identifying and evaluating exponents across multiple terms in the prime factorization expression.

###### Option 4
Targets students who add an extra factor when evaluating the expression. Computing $2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 8 \times 9 \times 5 = 360$ incorrectly increases the exponent on the factor of $2$ from $2$ to $3$, doubling the correct result and reflecting a systematic error in reading or applying exponents.
