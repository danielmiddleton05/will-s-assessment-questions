#### Task Description

**Given:** A manufacturing plant produces 600 units in a facility that occupies 5,000 square feet. The company plans to expand to a 7,500 square foot facility and expects production to scale proportionally.

How many units can the expanded facility produce?

##### Explanation
Set up a proportion: $\frac{600}{5,000} = \frac{x}{7,500}$

Cross-multiply: $5,000x = 600 \times 7,500 = 4,500,000$

Solve for $x$: $x = \frac{4,500,000}{5,000} = 900$

The facility space increases by a factor of 1.5 (from 5,000 to 7,500 square feet), so production scales proportionally: $600 \times 1.5 = 900$ units.

##### Distractors

###### Option 1
Results from assuming production remains constant despite the facility expansion. Student fails to recognize the proportional relationship between space and production capacity.

###### Option 2
Results from calculating the growth rate using the final facility size as the denominator rather than the original size. The student finds the increase in space (2,500 sq ft) and divides it by the new total (7,500 sq ft) to get a 1/3 increase, then incorrectly applies this 1/3 increase to the production capacity (600 + 200 = 800).

###### Option 4
Results from doubling the production ($600 \times 2 = 1,200$). Student may misread the expansion as doubling the facility size or incorrectly apply a 2x multiplier when the actual increase is 1.5x.
