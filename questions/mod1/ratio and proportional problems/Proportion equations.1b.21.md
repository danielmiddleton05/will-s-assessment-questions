#### Task Description

**Given:** A statistician is designing a stratified random sample from a population divided into two strata in a ratio of 3:7 (Stratum A to Stratum B). The required total sample size is 400 participants.

How many participants should be sampled from Stratum A to maintain proportional allocation?

##### Explanation
Total parts = $3 + 7 = 10$. Stratum A fraction = $\frac{3}{10}$. Number of participants from Stratum A = $\frac{3}{10} \times 400 = 120$ participants.

##### Distractors
###### Option 2
Applies an incorrect calculation method. Students may divide the total sample by one part of the ratio ($\frac{400}{3} \approx 133.33$), misunderstanding how to apply proportional allocation.

###### Option 3
Misinterprets the ratio as a direct fraction. Students may incorrectly compute $\frac{3}{7} \times 400 \approx 171.43$ and round to 171, treating 3:7 as $\frac{3}{7}$ rather than recognizing that total parts equal 10.

###### Option 4
Computes the allocation for Stratum B instead. Using $\frac{7}{10} \times 400 = 280$ gives the number for the other stratum, not Stratum A.
