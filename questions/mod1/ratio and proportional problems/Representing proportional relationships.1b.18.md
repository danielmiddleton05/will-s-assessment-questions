#### Task Description

**Given:** A tutoring center charges $60 for 4 hours of tutoring.

What equation represents the relationship between total cost ($c$) and hours of tutoring ($h$)?

#### Quiz Options

###### Option 1 Incorrect
$c = 4h$


###### Option 2 Correct
$c = 15h$


###### Option 3 Incorrect
$c = 60h$


###### Option 4 Incorrect
$c = 240h$

##### Explanation
To find the correct equation, calculate the hourly rate: \$60 ÷ 4 hours = \$15 per hour. Since total cost equals the hourly rate multiplied by the number of hours, the equation is $c = 15h$. This represents a proportional relationship where cost increases linearly with hours of tutoring.

##### Distractors
###### Option 1
$c = 4h$ — Inverts the rate calculation by using the number of hours as the coefficient. This targets students who recognize that a proportional relationship has the form $c = kh$ but select the wrong given value as the constant of proportionality, using 4 instead of calculating 60 ÷ 4.

###### Option 3
$c = 60h$ — Uses the total cost as the rate without finding the unit rate. This targets students who identify the dollar amount as relevant to the rate but fail to divide by the number of hours, treating the entire \$60 as the cost per hour rather than the cost for 4 hours.

###### Option 4
$c = 240h$ — Multiplies instead of divides to find the unit rate (60 × 4 = 240). This targets students who understand they need to combine the two given values to find the rate but apply the wrong operation, resulting in a rate that is 16 times too large.
